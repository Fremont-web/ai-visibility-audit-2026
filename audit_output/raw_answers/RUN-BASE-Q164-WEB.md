Independent repair shops cannot perform warranty repairs or officially clear manufacturer safety recalls at factory expense.

### Tips Before Scheduling Recall or Warranty Service

* **Check Your VIN First:** Look up your 17-digit Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) on the official [Mopar Recall Lookup](https://recalls.mopar.com/) or the [NHTSA Recalls Tool](https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls) to confirm open campaign numbers and repair descriptions.
* **Call Ahead to Verify Parts:** Recall remedies often require specific parts or software patches. Always contact the dealership's service department with your VIN ahead of time to confirm the necessary parts are physically on the shelf before dropping off your vehicle.
* **Weather & Highway Closures:** Because travel along I-80 and US-287 is subject to frequent seasonal weather closures and high-wind restrictions, utilizing the local Rawlins facility can save substantial highway driving time.
