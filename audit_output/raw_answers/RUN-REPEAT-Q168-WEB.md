### Important Tips Before Scheduling

* **Safety Recalls Are Free:** By law, all manufacturer safety recall repairs and software updates are performed free of charge to the vehicle owner.
* **Confirm Active Recalls by VIN:** Before booking, check your 17-digit Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) on the [NHTSA Recall Database](https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls) or [Ram Official Recall Portal](https://www.ramtrucks.com/webdirect/dop/recall.html) to identify specific recall campaign numbers.
* **Call Ahead to Verify Parts:** Some recalls require specialized remedy kits, sensors, or fuel hardware that dealerships order on an as-needed basis. Always call the service advisor in advance with your VIN so they can confirm the required parts are physically in stock before you make the trip.
