# Where in Wyoming to Compare AWD Family SUVs and Minivans Across Multiple Brands

Shopping for both all-wheel-drive (AWD) family SUVs and AWD minivans in Wyoming involves an important automotive reality: **the AWD minivan segment is extremely narrow**.

### 1. Crucial Market Context: The AWD Minivan Landscape
* **Only Two AWD Minivans Exist New in the U.S.:** Currently, the only new minivans offering all-wheel drive are the **Toyota Sienna** (hybrid-only with Electronic On-Demand AWD) and the **Chrysler Pacifica** (gas-powered V6 with mechanical AWD; note that the Pacifica Plug-in Hybrid is strictly FWD). Competitors such as the Honda Odyssey and Kia Carnival are front-wheel drive only.
* **AWD 3-Row Family SUVs are Ubiquitous:** By contrast, nearly every automaker offers AWD/4WD midsize and full-size family SUVs—including the Toyota Highlander/Grand Highlander, Honda Pilot, Ford Explorer, Chevrolet Traverse, GMC Acadia, Subaru Ascent, and Volkswagen Atlas.
* **The Strategy:** To cross-shop both AWD minivans and AWD family SUVs across several brands without endless driving, you need an automotive corridor or dealer group that includes **both a Toyota franchise and a Chrysler/Dodge/Jeep/Ram (CDJR) franchise**, alongside other major brands.

---

### 2. Top In-State Hubs for Multi-Brand Cross-Shopping

#### A. Cheyenne: Westland Road & Lincolnway Corridor (Best Overall In-State Cluster)
Cheyenne offers the highest concentration of major auto franchises in Wyoming, almost all clustered within a 1-to-2-mile radius along **Westland Road** and adjacent **Lincolnway**:
* **Ken Garff Toyota Cheyenne** *(2300 Westland Rd)*: The primary destination to evaluate the **Toyota Sienna AWD** against Toyota’s 3-row AWD SUVs, the **Highlander AWD** and **Grand Highlander AWD**.
* **Cowboy Dodge Chrysler Jeep Ram** *(1121 E Lincolnway)*: Located just minutes east on Lincolnway; here you can inspect the **Chrysler Pacifica AWD** alongside rugged 3-row SUVs like the **Jeep Grand Cherokee L (4WD)** and **Dodge Durango (AWD)**.
* **Ken Garff Ford Cheyenne** *(2200 Westland Rd)*: Adjacent to Toyota; offers the popular **Ford Explorer (AWD)** and full-size Expedition.
* **Tyrrell Honda** *(1919 Westland Rd)*: Down the street; useful for testing the **Honda Pilot (AWD)** and comparing minivan dimensions with the FWD Honda Odyssey.
* **Tyrrell Chevrolet** *(2142 W Lincolnway)*: Offers the 3-row **Chevrolet Traverse (AWD)** and Tahoe/Suburban.
* **Halladay Subaru** *(2030 Westland Rd)*: Carries the **Subaru Ascent (AWD)** with standard symmetrical AWD and 8.7 inches of ground clearance.
* **Halladay GMC / Cadillac** *(2100 Westland Rd)*: Carries the **GMC Acadia (AWD)** and Yukon.

*Shopper Advantage:* In Cheyenne, you can drive both of the market's only AWD minivans and compare them directly against five different brands of 3-row AWD SUVs in a single afternoon.

Casper is Central Wyoming's primary automotive commercial center, featuring tightly clustered brand dealerships:
* **East 2nd Street Strip (Sienna vs. Pacifica Side-by-Side):**
  * **Vista Toyota** *(6280 E 2nd St)*: Carries the Sienna AWD, Highlander AWD, and Grand Highlander AWD.

  * **Fremont Volkswagen of Casper** *(6001 E 2nd St)*: Located right in the same complex; carries the spacious 3-row **VW Atlas (AWD)**.
* **CY Avenue / West Casper:**
  * **Greiner Ford of Casper** *(3333 CY Ave)*: Ford Explorer AWD.
  * **Fremont Honda of Casper** *(3801 CY Ave)*: Honda Pilot AWD.
* **White's Mountain Motors** *(2400 E Yellowstone Hwy)*: Chevrolet Traverse AWD.

*Shopper Advantage:* East 2nd Street in Casper is unique because Vista Toyota and Fremont CDJR sit right next to each other, making an immediate side-by-side test drive of the Sienna AWD and Pacifica AWD exceptionally convenient.

### 3. Regional Alternatives for Expanded Inventory
Due to Wyoming's lower population and nationwide demand constraints—particularly on the hybrid **Toyota Sienna AWD**, which can see limited dealer allocations—many Wyoming buyers expand their search across state lines:
* **Northern Colorado (Fort Collins & Loveland — ~45–60 mins south of Cheyenne):**
  * **Motorplex at Centerra** *(I-25 & Crossroads Blvd, Loveland, CO)*: Northern Colorado's largest automotive hub, featuring CDJR, Chevrolet, Subaru, GMC, Hyundai, and a **CarMax** superstore for broad pre-owned comparison.
  * **College Avenue (Fort Collins, CO):** Home to high-volume dealers such as **Pedersen Toyota** and **Markley Motors** (Honda/Buick/GMC).
* **Billings, Montana (King Avenue West Auto Row):** Convenient for northern Wyoming shoppers (Sheridan, Buffalo, Gillette), featuring high-volume dealerships including Lithia CDJR and Denny Menholt Chevrolet.
* **Salt Lake City Metro / Ogden, Utah:** Serves southwestern Wyoming (Evanston, Green River, Rock Springs) with major multi-brand dealer clusters.

### 4. Practical Consumer Advice
1. **Call Ahead on Minivan Inventory:** New Toyota Sienna AWDs often sell before arriving on dealer lots. Call ahead to Cheyenne (Ken Garff Toyota), Casper (Vista Toyota), or Lander (Fremont Toyota) to ensure an AWD model is on the ground for a test drive.
2. **Drivetrain & Winter Clearance Considerations:**
   * **Toyota Sienna AWD:** Uses an independent electric rear motor; delivers outstanding fuel efficiency (~35 mpg combined), but ground clearance is minivan-standard (~6.3 inches).
   * **Chrysler Pacifica AWD:** Uses a mechanical driveshaft that disengages when not needed; traditional V6 power with ~5.4 inches of ground clearance.
   * **Family SUVs:** If deep Wyoming snow, unpaved rural roads, or high-drift mountain driving are frequent factors, 3-row SUVs like the Subaru Ascent, Ford Explorer, or Jeep Grand Cherokee L offer 8.0 to 8.7+ inches of clearance and dedicated terrain modes.
3. **Consider Multi-Brand Pre-Owned Lots:** If you want to test drive a used Sienna AWD, used Pacifica AWD, and several AWD SUVs parked in the exact same parking lot, visit dedicated pre-owned centers like **DriveWise** in Cheyenne or the pre-owned lots of large dealer groups like Fremont Motors.
