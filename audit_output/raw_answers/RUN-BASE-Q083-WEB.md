### Buyer Tips for Purchasing a Used Jeep Under Warranty

1. **Distinguish Factory CPO vs. Dealer/Aftermarket Warranties:** 
   * *Mopar Factory CPO* is honored at any authorized Jeep dealer nationwide, which is crucial if you travel or move.
   * *Dealer-specific warranties* (e.g., Fremont Certified) or third-party service contracts have distinct deductible rules, mileage limitations, and preferred repair facilities. Always read the fine print regarding what components (transfer case, suspension, electronics) are covered.
2. **Inspect for Off-Road Wear and Modifications:** 
   * Check the undercarriage, skid plates, differential covers, and frame for rock rash, mud packing, or rust.
   * Verify whether aftermarket modifications (e.g., aftermarket lift kits, oversized tires, tuners) affect or void the remaining manufacturer or extended warranty coverage.
3. **Request History and Maintenance Records:** 
   * Ask for a CARFAX or AutoCheck report to confirm single-owner history, regular servicing, and no prior accident or flood damage.
