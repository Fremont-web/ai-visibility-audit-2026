**Question:** What are the best Chrysler dealerships in or near Cody, Wyoming, and why?

**SHA-256 Hash:** `None`

---

For shoppers in or around Cody, Wyoming, dealership options reflect the rural geography of the Bighorn Basin. Because Chrysler is sold under the Stellantis Chrysler Dodge Jeep Ram (CDJR) franchise network—and its current lineup is focused primarily on the Pacifica minivan—options range from a single authorized local dealer in Cody proper to neighboring Park County and regional metro alternatives.

---

### 1. Primary Local Dealership: Fremont CDJR Cody (Fremont Motor Cody)
* **Location:** 3127 Big Horn Ave, Cody, WY 82414
* **Designation:** Local
* **Why It’s a Top Choice:**
  * **In-Town Convenience:** It is the only franchised Chrysler dealership operating directly in Cody, making it the most convenient option for routine maintenance, warranty work, and factory recalls.
  * **Statewide Network Transfers:** As part of Fremont Motor Companies (one of Wyoming’s largest dealer groups), the Cody team can transfer vehicles from sister lots across Wyoming if a specific trim or package is not sitting on the Cody lot.
  * **Fremont Care Program:** Qualifying new and pre-owned purchases include Fremont's bundled maintenance program (such as oil changes and roadside support), adding value for local drivers.

---

### 2. Closest Neighboring Alternative: Fremont Motor Powell
* **Location:** 1000 US Highway 14A, Powell, WY 82435 (~24 miles northeast of Cody)
* **Designation:** Local / Neighboring Area
* **Why It’s a Good Option:**
  * **Short Drive:** Located roughly 25 to 30 minutes away in neighboring Powell, this full-line CDJR and Ford dealership offers an easy second local option within Park County.
  * **Shared Group Inventory:** Like the Cody location, Powell is part of the Fremont Motor family. If the Cody store is busy or lacks a particular vehicle, cross-shopping at Powell allows buyers to check alternate lot inventory or service department scheduling without traveling far.

---

### 3. Regional High-Volume Option: Lithia Chrysler Jeep Dodge of Billings
* **Location:** 2229 King Ave W, Billings, MT 59102 (~90–100 miles north of Cody)
* **Designation:** Regional
* **Why Consider It:**
  * **Greater Inventory Volume:** As a high-volume dealership located in the primary metropolitan commerce hub for the region, Lithia Billings typically stocks a wider selection of new Chrysler Pacifica and Pacifica Plug-in Hybrid models than smaller rural lots.
  * **Competitive Regional Pricing:** High turnover and larger allocation frequently allow for aggressive discounting or unique manufacturer incentives.
  * **Trade-off:** Driving to Billings requires roughly 1.5 to 2 hours each way, and Wyoming buyers purchasing in Montana must coordinate out-of-state paperwork to pay Wyoming county sales tax and register the vehicle locally.

---

### Other Regional Considerations
* **Sheridan Motors CDJR** (1858 Coffeen Ave, Sheridan, WY 82801; ~145 miles east via US-14/I-90) and **Fremont CDJR Lander** (1700 E Main St, Lander, WY 82520; ~130 miles south) serve as secondary Wyoming alternatives, though they generally require longer mountain or rural highway drives compared to Billings or Powell.

---

### Shopper Tips
* **Call Ahead to Verify Stock:** Because Chrysler's standalone lineup currently consists of the Pacifica and Pacifica Plug-in Hybrid, local rural allocations can be limited. Check online inventory or call before visiting.
* **Service & Warranty Portability:** Any warranty or factory recall work can be handled at any authorized Chrysler/CDJR dealership, meaning you can buy in Billings or Powell and still service the vehicle locally at Fremont CDJR Cody.
