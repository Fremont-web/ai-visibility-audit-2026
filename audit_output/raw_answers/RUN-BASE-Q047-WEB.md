### Shopper Checklist When Buying a Warrantied Used Ford:
1. **Verify the Certification Tier:** Ask specifically if the vehicle is **Ford Blue Advantage Gold Certified** (the gold standard for used Fords), **Blue Certified**, or covered solely by a dealer/third-party service contract.
2. **Check Remaining Factory Warranty:** Newer used Fords (under 3 years/36,000 miles bumper-to-bumper or 5 years/60,000 miles powertrain) often have transferable factory coverage remaining regardless of where you buy.
3. **Review Vehicle History:** Always request a full CARFAX or AutoCheck report showing past ownership, service records, and title status before finalizing your purchase.
4. **Clarify Terms & Deductibles:** Review the warranty agreement to verify what components are covered (powertrain only vs. comprehensive bumper-to-bumper), deductible amounts, and whether repair work must be performed at the selling dealership or can be serviced at any authorized Ford facility.
