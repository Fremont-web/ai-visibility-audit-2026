### Important Consumer Tips for Warranty & Recall Service

* **Authorized Dealership Requirement:** Federal safety recalls and manufacturer limited warranty repairs must be performed by an authorized franchised Dodge/Mopar dealer to be covered at no charge. Independent repair shops cannot perform warranty or recall work free of charge under factory coverage.
* **Verify Open Recalls Ahead of Time:** Have your 17-digit Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) ready and check the official [NHTSA Recall Website](https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls) or the [Mopar Recall Portal](https://www.mopar.com/en-us/my-vehicle/recalls.html) to confirm any open safety recalls and note specific campaign numbers.
* **Call Ahead for Parts and Staffing:** Because some recall remedies require specialized replacement parts kits (such as fuel pumps, airbags, or steering components) or certified master technicians, calling the service department in advance ensures parts are physically in stock before you drop off your vehicle.
