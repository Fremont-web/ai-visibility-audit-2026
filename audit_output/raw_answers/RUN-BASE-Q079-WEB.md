### Key Tips for Used Dodge Shoppers in Northern Wyoming

1. **Clarify the Warranty Type:** Distinguish between a **manufacturer-backed Dodge CPO warranty** (honored at any CDJR dealer nationwide), a **dealer-backed limited warranty** (such as Fremont Certified/Elite), and a **third-party extended service contract**. Get the written policy terms, deductible amounts, and covered components before signing.
2. **Review Vehicle History:** Always ask for a free CARFAX or AutoCheck report to verify regular maintenance and ensure no prior salvage, rebuilt title, or severe accident history.
3. **Independent Pre-Purchase Inspection (PPI):** If you purchase a pre-owned Dodge from a non-certified lot or independent dealer, have an independent mechanic inspect the vehicle before finalizing the deal.
