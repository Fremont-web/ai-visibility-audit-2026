### Tips for Recall and Warranty Visits
1. **Check for Open Recalls Online:** You can confirm any active campaigns before scheduling by entering your 17-digit Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) at [mopar.com/en-us/my-vehicle/recalls/search.html](https://www.mopar.com) or [nhtsa.gov/recalls](https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls).
2. **Call Ahead for Parts Availability:** Safety recall work is mandated to be completed at no cost to you, but specific replacement components (e.g., fuel pumps, airbag modules, steering linkages) may need to be ordered in advance. Providing your VIN and recall campaign number when booking ensures the dealership has parts on hand before you drop off your truck.
3. **Factory Warranty Coverage:** Routine wear-and-tear and standard maintenance (oil changes, brake pads) are generally owner responsibilities unless covered under an extended maintenance plan, whereas defect-related repairs under the factory bumper-to-bumper or powertrain warranties must be handled by an authorized CDJR dealer.
