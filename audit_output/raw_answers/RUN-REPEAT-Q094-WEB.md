If you are looking to purchase a new or pre-owned Ford F-150 in or near Sheridan, Wyoming, you have both a dedicated local franchise dealership and several regional alternatives within a comfortable driving distance.

---

### 1. Local Dealership in Sheridan, WY

* **Fremont Ford Sheridan**
  * **Location:** 1658 Coffeen Avenue, Sheridan, WY 82801
  * **Contact:** (307) 763-5621 / (307) 683-4070
  * **Details:** Fremont Ford Sheridan is the sole authorized, franchised Ford dealer directly in Sheridan County. They carry new Ford F-150 inventory across standard trims (XL, XLT, Lariat, King Ranch, Platinum, Tremor) as well as certified pre-owned and used trucks. In addition, because they are part of Fremont Motor Companies, sales consultants can often arrange inventory transfers from sister Fremont dealerships across Wyoming.

### 3. Pre-Owned F-150 Options in Sheridan

If you are shopping strictly for a used Ford F-150, local non-Ford franchised dealerships such as Sheridan Motors (CDJR and GMC) and Wareing Sheridan Chevrolet regularly stock pre-owned Ford F-150s acquired through trade-ins. Additionally, Prime Rate Motors in Sheridan is an independent dealer that frequently inventories pre-owned trucks, including used Ford F-150 models.

### Important Shopper Tips

1. **Out-of-State Purchases (Montana):** While Montana does not levy a state retail sales tax, Wyoming residents buying in Montana will still need to pay standard Wyoming state and county sales tax when registering and titling the vehicle at the Sheridan County Treasurer's office.
2. **Inventory Verification:** F-150 inventory moves quickly in truck-heavy markets; call ahead or confirm current window stickers and pricing on dealer websites before traveling.
