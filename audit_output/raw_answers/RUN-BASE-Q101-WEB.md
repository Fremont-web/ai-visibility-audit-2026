### Tips for Buying a Used Car in Sheridan
1.  **Inspect for Rust & Underbody Wear:** Wyoming winters mean road de-icing chemicals and gravel exposure. Always examine the frame and suspension components.
2.  **Verify History & CARFAX:** Reputable dealers in Sheridan provide complimentary vehicle history reports showing previous ownership, service history, and whether the car was a local or out-of-state vehicle.
3.  **Check Documentation Fees:** Dealership document fees in the Sheridan area typically range between $499 and $599; ensure these are clearly disclosed on your purchase agreement.
