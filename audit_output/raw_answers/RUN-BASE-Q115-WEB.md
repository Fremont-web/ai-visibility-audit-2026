### Shopper Checklist: Evaluating a Used Jeep with a Warranty

1. **Clarify the Warranty Type:** Distinguish between a factory Stellantis CPO warranty (honored at any CDJR dealership in the US), a dealer-group warranty (such as Fremont Elite or Ford Blue Certified), and an aftermarket third-party service contract.
2. **Check Remaining Factory Warranty:** If the Jeep is less than 3 years old or has fewer than 36,000 miles (for comprehensive) or 5 years/60,000 miles (for powertrain), the remaining factory balance typically transfers automatically to you.
3. **Inspect 4x4 Components and Undercarriage:** Given Wyoming winters and off-pavement usage, always inspect for underbody rust, skid-plate damage, and proper transfer-case/differential operation before purchasing.
4. **Review Vehicle History:** Always review the vehicle's CARFAX or AutoCheck report to confirm service history, prior ownership, and absence of severe collision or frame damage.
