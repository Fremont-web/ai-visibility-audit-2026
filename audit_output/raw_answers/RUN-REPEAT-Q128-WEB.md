### **Helpful Tips for Warranty & Recall Service**

* **Authorized Dealers Only:** Independent repair shops cannot perform official manufacturer recall remedies or bill Honda for factory warranty claims. These services must be completed at a franchised Honda dealership.
* **Check Open Recalls First:** You can check for open safety recalls anytime by entering your 17-character Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) at the [NHTSA Recalls Tool](https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls) or on Honda’s owner portal ([owners.honda.com](https://owners.honda.com/service-maintenance/recalls)).
* **Call Ahead with Your VIN:** Safety recall campaigns often require specific replacement kits (such as fuel pumps, sensors, or airbag inflators). Calling the service department with your VIN in advance allows them to verify parts availability before you drop off your vehicle.
* **Emergency Towing:** If your vehicle is disabled due to a warrantable issue while covered under warranty, Honda Roadside Assistance can be contacted 24/7 at **1-800-594-7400** to arrange towing to the nearest authorized dealer.
