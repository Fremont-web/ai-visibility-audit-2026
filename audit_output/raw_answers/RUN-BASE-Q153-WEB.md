1. **Current Chrysler Lineup:** Chrysler’s standalone brand lineup currently centers on minivans (Pacifica and Pacifica Plug-in Hybrid). As a result, dealer lots generally carry fewer standalone Chrysler units compared to Ram trucks or Jeep SUVs. Check current inventory online or call ahead before visiting.
2. **Network Inventory Transfers:** If you prefer to buy locally in Rawlins, inquire with Fremont CDJR Rawlins whether a vehicle from their Casper or Rock Springs branches can be transferred for local delivery.
3. **Seasonal Travel Realities:** Traveling from Rawlins to Laramie, Casper, or Rock Springs requires navigating high-altitude highway corridors (I-80 Elk Mountain / Arlington stretch) that are frequently subject to closures, blowing snow, and high-wind restrictions in winter months. Contact dealerships by phone or email first to negotiate details before traveling.
