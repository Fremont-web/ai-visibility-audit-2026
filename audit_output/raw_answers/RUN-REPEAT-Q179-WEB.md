### How Automotive Warranty Jurisdiction Works

When navigating warranty work across multi-location dealership groups, shoppers should understand the distinction between **Manufacturer (OEM) Warranties**, **Dealer Group In-House Warranties**, and **Third-Party Extended Warranties**:

* **Factory (OEM) Warranties & Safety Recalls:** Under federal franchise regulations and manufacturer warranty agreements, factory warranty repairs, powertrain warranties, OEM technical service bulletins (TSBs), and federal safety recalls **must be performed by an authorized, franchised dealership for that specific make**. A dealer group cannot process factory warranty claims for a brand it is not franchised to sell at that specific facility. For instance, a Ford factory recall cannot be billed or performed at a standalone Chrysler or Toyota rooftop within the same group.
* **In-House Dealer Group Warranties & Maintenance Plans:** Dealership network perks—such as **Fremont Care** (complimentary 1-year maintenance including oil changes, tire rotations, and inspections) or Fremont Certified Pre-Owned limited warranties—can be redeemed at **any service center within that dealer group statewide**, regardless of the vehicle make.
* **Third-Party Vehicle Service Contracts (VSC):** Extended warranties purchased through independent administrators (e.g., Zurich, Ally, CNA, Fidelity) are accepted at **any certified repair shop** across both groups' locations, provided the repair facility contacts the contract administrator for pre-authorization before work begins.
* **Routine Customer-Pay Maintenance:** Any service center in these groups (including Fremont's Quick Lane Tire & Auto Centers) can perform general customer-pay upkeep (oil, brakes, batteries, tires) on any vehicle make.

### Summary Advice for Consumers
When scheduling warranty work in Wyoming, verify whether your repair is a **factory-covered issue/recall** or an **extended/maintenance contract**:
1. If it is a **manufacturer warranty claim or recall**, you must take the vehicle to a dealership specifically franchised for your vehicle make (e.g., Fremont Ford for a Ford recall, or White's Energy Motors for a Ram recall).
2. If you are using **group maintenance perks** (like *Fremont Care*) or an **independent extended service contract**, you can schedule service at any facility within the group's multi-city network.
