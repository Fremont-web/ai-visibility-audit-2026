### Consumer Tips for Recall and Warranty Visits

1. **Safety Recalls Are Free:** By federal law, safety recall repairs must be completed at **no cost** to the vehicle owner when serviced at an authorized franchised dealer.
2. **Check Open Recalls First:** Look up open campaigns by entering your 17-digit Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) on the [Mopar Recall Lookup](https://www.mopar.com/en-us/my-vehicle/recalls.html) or [NHTSA Recalls](https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls).
3. **Call Ahead with Your VIN:** Always provide your VIN when booking an appointment so the service department can ensure the required replacement parts and software patches are physically in stock before you bring the car in.
