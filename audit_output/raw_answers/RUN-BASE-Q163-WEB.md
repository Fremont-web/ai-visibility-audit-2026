### 3. Understanding Used Jeep Warranty Types

When evaluating a used Jeep, understand which level of coverage applies:
1. **Remaining Factory Warranty:** If the Jeep is relatively new, the balance of the original 3-year/36,000-mile basic warranty and 5-year/60,000-mile powertrain warranty transfers automatically to subsequent owners. Have the dealer run the VIN through their database to confirm start and expiration dates.
2. **Manufacturer Certified Pre-Owned (Mopar / Stellantis CPO):** Provides the highest peace of mind because warranty claims can be serviced at any authorized Jeep dealership nationwide with zero dispute over OEM parts.
3. **Dealer-Backed Limited Warranties:** Programs like Fremont Certified or CarBravo protect major mechanical items (engine, transmission, drive axles) for a designated duration/mileage, but usually feature specific component exclusions.
4. **Mopar Vehicle Protection (MVP):** You can also purchase an official factory-backed extended service contract directly through any authorized CDJR dealer (or reputable online Stellantis dealers) before or during purchase.

### 4. Shopper Recommendations for Wyoming Conditions
* **Inspect the Undercarriage:** Jeeps are popular for trail and off-road use; ensure a certified technician checks for underbody rock damage, suspension wear, and transfer case health.
* **Confirm Winter Equipment:** Verify that the 4WD system shifts smoothly into 4-Hi and 4-Lo, and check that winter essentials (block heater, heated mirrors/seats, battery health) are in good condition for Carbon County winters.
* **Review the Fine Print:** For any warranty or service contract, verify the deductible (typically $0 to $100 per visit), whether rental car allowances are provided during repairs, and whether towing assistance is included.
