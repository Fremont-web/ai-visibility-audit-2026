**Question:** What are the best Ford dealerships in or near Sheridan, Wyoming, and why?

For car shoppers in Sheridan, Wyoming, dealership options depend heavily on whether you need immediate local service or are willing to travel along Interstate 90 for a wider inventory selection.

Because Sheridan is a smaller market, there is only **one** authorized Ford franchise dealership located directly in town. Shoppers frequently compare it against regional Ford dealerships located roughly 70 to 130 miles away in neighboring Wyoming and Montana communities.

### Consumer Tips for Sheridan Shoppers

* **Cross-Border Tax Considerations:** If you purchase a vehicle in Montana (which has no state sales tax), remember that Wyoming residents must still pay state and local county sales tax when titling and registering the vehicle at their county treasurer's office in Wyoming.
