**Question:** What are the best Jeep dealerships in or near Cody, Wyoming, and why?

When shopping for a Jeep in or around Cody, Wyoming, your choices are shaped by the rural geography of the Big Horn Basin. There is only one authorized, franchised Jeep dealership located directly in Cody, but shoppers have strong nearby and regional alternatives within driving distance depending on whether local service convenience or larger inventory selection is the priority.

### 1. Fremont Chrysler Dodge Jeep Ram (Cody, WY) – The Primary Local Option
* **Location:** 3127 Big Horn Avenue, Cody, WY 82414
* **Role:** Local (In-town)
* **Why It’s a Top Choice:** As the only factory-authorized Jeep dealer physically located in Cody, Fremont CDJR is the most convenient option for local test drives, warranty repairs, Mopar factory parts, and routine maintenance. Because it is part of the statewide Fremont Motor Companies group, the sales team can frequently transfer vehicles located at other Fremont lots across Wyoming, expanding your selection beyond what sits on the Cody lot.
* **Shopper Caveats:** As with many rural franchise dealerships, lot inventory can be limited for specialized trims or configurations. Customer reviews on platforms like DealerRater and Google generally praise the sales staff, though experiences with service department turnaround times and communication can vary.

### 2. Fremont Motor Powell (Powell, WY) – The Closest Neighboring Alternative
* **Location:** 1000 US Highway 14A, Powell, WY 82435 (~24 miles northeast of Cody)
* **Role:** Local / Nearby
* **Why It’s a Top Choice:** Located roughly 25 to 30 minutes away in neighboring Powell, this sister Fremont location sells and services Chrysler, Dodge, Jeep, Ram, and Ford vehicles. It consistently earns high customer satisfaction ratings (averaging around 4.8 out of 5 stars on DealerRater) and provides a friendly, small-town buying experience. For Cody residents, it serves as an easy secondary option for service scheduling or finding a specific vehicle in stock without traveling far.
* **Shopper Caveats:** Because it shares ownership with Fremont Cody, general pricing policies and backend systems are aligned. Independent consumer reports note that getting binding, written out-the-door price quotes remotely can sometimes require direct phone follow-up or an in-person visit.

### 3. Lithia Chrysler Jeep Dodge RAM of Billings (Billings, MT) – The Regional Volume Hub
* **Location:** 2229 King Avenue West, Billings, MT 59102 (~100 miles north of Cody)
* **Role:** Regional
* **Why It’s a Top Choice:** Billings is the main commercial and automotive hub for northern Wyoming and south-central Montana. Lithia CDJR maintains a much larger vehicle inventory than smaller Basin dealerships, making it the best option if you are hunting for specific Wrangler or Gladiator configurations, high-end Grand Cherokee trims, or 4xe hybrid models. As a high-volume dealer, they may also offer broader manufacturer incentive options and promotional pricing.
* **Shopper Caveats:** Making the nearly two-hour drive means routine warranty work and maintenance visits are less convenient than staying local. Additionally, while Montana does not have a state sales tax, Wyoming buyers must still pay Wyoming state and Park County sales taxes when titling and registering the vehicle back home.

### Additional Options to Keep in Mind
* **Independent Pre-Owned:** If you are shopping for a used Wrangler or Grand Cherokee rather than new, Whitlock Motors in Cody is an established independent dealer that frequently carries pre-owned Jeeps without the franchise markup.
* **East of the Bighorns:** Sheridan Motors CDJR in Sheridan, Wyoming (~145 miles east) is another regional option, though driving over the Bighorn Mountains via US-14 can be weather-dependent during winter months.

### Practical Recommendations for Shoppers
1. **Check Fremont Network Inventory First:** Before driving out of state, ask Fremont CDJR in Cody or Powell whether the exact trim or package you want can be transferred from another Fremont store in Wyoming.
2. **Compare Out-the-Door (OTD) Pricing in Writing:** Request a transparent breakdown including doc fees, taxes, and any dealer-installed accessories before committing to a purchase.
3. **Plan for Service Convenience:** Even if you buy from a volume dealer in Billings to secure a specific spec or deal, local warranty work, recall campaigns, and scheduled maintenance can still be performed locally at Fremont CDJR in Cody or Powell.
