### Important Tips Before Scheduling Recall & Warranty Service
1. **Verify Open Recalls:** Safety recall work is 100% free under federal law, regardless of vehicle age, mileage, or where you bought the vehicle. Before booking, check your 17-digit Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) on [nhtsa.gov/recalls](https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls) or the official Mopar recall site.
2. **Call Ahead for Recall Parts:** Some recall campaigns require specialized parts or kits that dealers do not keep on shelves continuously. Call the service desk with your VIN so they can order and verify parts arrival before you drop off your vehicle.
3. **Warranty Verification:** Factory warranties (3-year/36,000-mile Basic Limited, 5-year/60,000-mile Powertrain, or active Mopar Vehicle Protection plans) can be pulled up directly by service advisors using your VIN.
