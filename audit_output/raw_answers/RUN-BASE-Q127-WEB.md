### Important Shopper Considerations in Wyoming
1. **Wyoming Used Car Lemon Laws:** Wyoming does not have a used-car lemon law. Unless a vehicle is sold with an express written warranty or remaining manufacturer warranty, it is legally sold "as-is." Always confirm warranty terms in writing on the FTC Buyer's Guide window sticker before signing.
2. **Manufacturer vs. Dealer Warranties:** A manufacturer-backed **HondaTrue** warranty is honored at any authorized Honda dealership nationwide, whereas dealer-specific warranties or third-party service contracts may require service at specific locations or incur repair deductibles.
3. **Vehicle History & Pre-Purchase Inspection:** Reputable dealerships in Casper provide free CARFAX or AutoCheck reports. Even when buying a certified or warrantied vehicle, reviewing maintenance records and having a trusted mechanic inspect the vehicle is recommended.
