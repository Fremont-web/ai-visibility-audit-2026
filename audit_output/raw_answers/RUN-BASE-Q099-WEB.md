### Practical Buyer Tips for Sheridan Shoppers

1. **Verify the Warranty Type:** Always ask whether a "warranty" is an official **Toyota Certified Used Vehicle (TCUV)** warranty honored at any Toyota dealer nationwide, a short-term dealer limited warranty (e.g., 3 months / 3,000 miles), or a third-party vehicle service contract with a deductible.
2. **Inspect Underbody for Winter Corrosion:** In Wyoming and Montana, winter road treatments (magnesium chloride and salt) can cause undercarriage corrosion. Have the frame and underside inspected, particularly on used trucks (Tacoma/Tundra) and SUVs (4Runner).
3. **Request Vehicle History:** Ensure the dealer provides a free CARFAX or AutoCheck report showing regular oil change intervals and clean title status.
4. **Out-of-State Purchasing (Montana):** If you purchase across state lines in Billings, remember that while Montana has no state sales tax, Wyoming residents must pay applicable Wyoming state and Sheridan County sales tax when registering the vehicle with the Sheridan County Treasurer.
