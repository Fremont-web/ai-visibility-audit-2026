### Buyer Tips for Buying a Warrantied Used Dodge in Wyoming

1. **Verify the Warranty Type in Writing:** Ensure you clearly understand whether your coverage is:
   * **Manufacturer CPO:** Backed by Dodge/Stellantis and honored at any CDJR dealership nationwide.
   * **Dealer Limited Warranty:** Backed by the dealer network (e.g., Fremont Elite).
   * **Remaining Factory Warranty:** The balance of the original 3-year/36,000-mile basic or 5-year/60,000-mile powertrain coverage.
   * **Aftermarket Service Contract:** A third-party policy requiring specific claim approval procedures and deductibles.
2. **Wyoming Has No Used-Car Lemon Law:** Wyoming's lemon law applies only to new vehicles. Once you drive off the lot with an "as-is" car, you are responsible for all repairs. Never rely on verbal promises; ensure every warranty term, deductible, and coverage limitation is explicitly detailed in your purchase paperwork.
3. **Independent Pre-Purchase Inspection (PPI):** Even on warrantied or certified vehicles, having an independent, trusted local mechanic inspect the vehicle before purchase can identify wear-and-tear items (brakes, suspension, tires) that mechanical breakdown warranties generally do not cover.
4. **Review the CARFAX/AutoCheck Report:** Verify regular oil change records, ownership history, and check for title issues or prior accident damage.
