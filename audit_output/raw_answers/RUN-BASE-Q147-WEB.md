### 3. Understanding Used Ram Warranty Options

When shopping for a used Ram with a warranty, determine which category of coverage the truck carries:

1.  **Remaining Factory Warranty:** Ram trucks come from the factory with a 3-year/36,000-mile basic warranty and a 5-year/60,000-mile powertrain warranty (extended to 5 years/100,000 miles for Cummins turbo-diesel engines). Any remaining balance transfers automatically to subsequent owners.
2.  **Ram Certified Pre-Owned (CPO):** Only available at authorized CDJR franchises (such as Fremont in Rock Springs or Uinta in Evanston). Includes factory-backed 7-year/100,000-mile powertrain coverage, 3-month/3,000-mile comprehensive coverage, CARFAX vehicle history report, and roadside assistance ($100 deductible per repair visit).
3.  **Dealer & Third-Party Service Contracts:** Non-CPO used trucks may come with a dealer-specific warranty (such as Fremont Elite or a dealer lifetime powertrain warranty) or an optional third-party vehicle service contract. Review the contract carefully to check whether repairs can be performed at any ASE-certified mechanic or must be serviced at the selling dealer.

### Recommendations for Shoppers

*   **Inspect the FTC Buyer’s Guide:** Check the window sticker to confirm whether the vehicle is sold "As-Is" or with a "Warranty" (and whether it is manufacturer or dealer-provided).
*   **Request Vehicle History:** Ensure the dealer provides a free CARFAX or AutoCheck report showing a clean title and documented maintenance history.
*   **Review Cummins Diesel Specifics:** If purchasing a heavy-duty diesel, confirm that emissions equipment and fuel systems are covered under the warranty terms, as these are common high-cost repair areas.
