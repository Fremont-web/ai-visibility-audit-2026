### Consumer Tips for Buying a Used Dodge with a Warranty

1. **Distinguish Between CPO, Dealer-Backed, and Third-Party Contracts:**
   * **Factory CPO (Stellantis):** Backed directly by Dodge/Mopar and honored at any authorized Dodge dealer nationwide.
   * **Dealer-Certified (e.g., Fremont Elite/Certified):** Backed by the dealer network; verify whether covered repairs must be performed at dealer-affiliated shops.
   * **Lifetime or Third-Party Warranties:** Review the policy agreement carefully for restrictions, such as strict requirements that oil changes be performed exclusively at specific facilities.
2. **Check Remaining Factory Warranty:** If the Dodge is less than 5 years old and under 60,000 miles, the remaining factory powertrain warranty may still be active and fully transferable at no additional cost.
3. **Review Vehicle History & Inspection:** Always request a CARFAX or AutoCheck report to confirm title status, prior maintenance, and that no open recalls exist on the vehicle.
