For shoppers in Powell, Wyoming, finding the right Ford dealership comes down to balancing local proximity against regional inventory selection. Dealership options naturally divide into two categories: **local Big Horn Basin dealerships** (within 25 miles) and **regional high-volume alternatives** in south-central Montana (roughly 75 to 90 miles north).

---

### 1. Local Dealerships (Powell and Cody Area)

#### **Fremont Motor Powell** – *Powell, WY (Local)*
* **Location:** 1000 US Highway 14A, Powell, WY 82435
* **Why it's a top choice:** As the only authorized Ford dealership situated directly within Powell, it offers unmatched day-to-day convenience for Powell residents. It is a full-service franchise providing new and pre-owned Ford vehicles, certified warranty work, routine maintenance, genuine Motorcraft parts, and an on-site collision center/body shop. Furthermore, as part of the Fremont Motor Companies network, it can coordinate inventory transfers from sister dealerships across Wyoming.
* **Shopper considerations:** The Powell location is a multi-franchise dealership (sharing lot and service capacity with Chrysler, Dodge, Jeep, and Ram), meaning standalone Ford inventory on the ground may be more modest than dedicated stores. Customer reviews generally commend quick routine service and communication, though shoppers should note that written out-the-door pricing quotes often require direct telephone or in-person contact rather than automated digital tools.

#### **Fremont Ford Cody** – *Cody, WY (Near Local / ~24 miles away)*
* **Location:** 3127 Big Horn Ave, Cody, WY 82414
* **Why it's a top choice:** Located approximately 25 to 30 minutes southwest of Powell, Fremont Ford Cody is a dedicated, standalone Ford dealership. It provides a focused Ford shopping experience with a broader dedicated inventory of F-Series pickups, Broncos, and commercial work trucks. The store enjoys strong consumer feedback for sales transparency (earning top transparency ratings on consumer auto portals), respectful sales staff, and a straightforward buying process.
* **Shopper considerations:** Because both the Powell and Cody locations belong to the Fremont Motor Companies dealer group, they share core systems and inventory databases. While this makes vehicle transfers straightforward, it means cross-shopping between the two stores may not yield significantly different pricing structures.

### 2. Regional Alternatives (South-Central Montana)

For shoppers seeking larger inventories, specific commercial packages, or a completely independent dealer network to cross-shop, heading north into Montana offers two prominent choices:

#### **Archie Cochrane Ford** – *Billings, MT (Regional / ~85–90 miles north)*
* **Location:** 2133 King Ave W, Billings, MT 59102
* **Why it's a top choice:** Operating since 1945, Archie Cochrane Ford is one of the largest-volume Ford franchises in the Northern Rockies region. Its substantial inventory depth is particularly valuable for shoppers looking for specialized F-150 or Super Duty configurations, specific trim levels, or commercial vehicle options. It maintains consistently high consumer review scores (typically around 4.8 out of 5 stars) and features a dedicated commercial vehicle and fleet service facility.
* **Shopper considerations:** Reaching Billings requires a drive of roughly 1 hour and 25 minutes north via US-310 and I-90. However, for buyers wanting maximum on-lot selection or high-volume pricing competition, the trip can be worthwhile.

#### **Laurel Ford** – *Laurel, MT (Regional / ~75 miles north)*
* **Location:** 500 SE 4th St, Laurel, MT 59044
* **Why it's a top choice:** Situated just southwest of Billings along the driving route from Powell, Laurel Ford offers a customer-friendly, low-pressure sales atmosphere. It has earned strong marks for pricing transparency (minimal dealer add-ons and clear pricing) and reliable service department support, making it an appealing regional alternative.
* **Shopper considerations:** Total inventory is smaller than Archie Cochrane’s mega-lot in Billings, but Laurel Ford offers an independent alternative closer than Billings proper.

1. **Group Inventory Access:** If Fremont Motor Powell does not have your desired trim or configuration on the lot, ask sales representatives to check regional inventory across the Fremont group (including Cody, Lander, and Riverton), as dealer transfers can often be arranged locally.
