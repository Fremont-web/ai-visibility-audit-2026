### Commercial Work Truck Dealerships in Wyoming (Flatbeds & Service Bodies)

In Wyoming, commercial work trucks equipped with flatbeds or service/utility bodies are distributed through two primary channels: **light-to-medium-duty franchised commercial vehicle dealers** (Ford, RAM, Chevrolet, GMC) and **medium-to-heavy-duty commercial truck centers** (Freightliner, Western Star, International). 

Because many commercial buyers require specific body configurations (e.g., Knapheide, CM Truck Beds, Reading, Hillsboro, or Bedrock), dealerships either carry **pre-upfitted turnkey units** directly on their lots or maintain **chassis cab allocations** (Ford F-350 to F-600, RAM 3500 to 5500, Chevy Silverado 3500HD to 5500HD) coordinated with regional upfitters.

### Buyer Tips for Wyoming Commercial Shoppers
1. **Chassis Cab vs. Pickup Bed Removal:** If you need a heavy utility service body or a commercial gooseneck flatbed, start with a purpose-built **Chassis Cab** (flat frame rails, standardized cab-to-axle dimensions, dual rear wheels, and optional PTO) rather than removing the bed from a standard pickup.
2. **Turnkey vs. Upfit Order:** Ask the commercial sales manager whether the truck on the lot has final-stage FMVSS certification (ready to drive away) or if they need to send an unfinished chassis cab to an upfitter like Basin Upfitting or A-TEC.
3. **Commercial Programs:** Leverage manufacturer fleet programs (RAM BusinessLink, Ford Pro, GM Business Elite) for commercial lending, upfit cash allowances, expedited service scheduling, and Section 179 vehicle tax deductions.
