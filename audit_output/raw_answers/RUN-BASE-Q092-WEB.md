**Question:** Which dealerships near Powell, Wyoming, will buy my car without requiring me to buy another one?

If you want to sell your vehicle in or near Powell, Wyoming, without purchasing a replacement vehicle, several franchised and independent dealerships offer outright cash purchase programs (often called "We Buy Cars" or "Sell Us Your Car"). You are under no obligation to trade in or buy another car.

### Dealerships in Powell, WY

* **Fremont Motor Powell** (Ford, Chrysler, Dodge, Jeep, Ram)
  * **Location:** 1000 US Highway 14A, Powell, WY 82435
  * **Details:** Fremont Motor Powell runs an explicit "We Buy Cars" program. They provide online valuation tools and on-site appraisals, and they clearly state that they will purchase your vehicle for cash even if you do not buy a replacement vehicle from their lot.

* **Yellowstone Motors** (Yellowstone Chevrolet GMC & Yellowstone Toyota)
  * **Location:** 1105 W Coulter Ave, Powell, WY 82435 *(formerly Garvin Motors)*
  * **Details:** Yellowstone Motors operates multi-brand franchises on Coulter Avenue and actively buys pre-owned vehicles from private sellers. They offer cash buyout appraisals with zero requirement to trade in or purchase another vehicle.

### Practical Tips for an Outright Sale
1. **Bring Required Paperwork:** Have the vehicle title (free of liens), current registration, valid government ID, all sets of vehicle keys/remotes, and loan payoff details (if you still owe money on the car).
2. **Get Multiple Appraisals:** Dealership offers can vary based on their current used inventory needs; stopping by both Fremont and Yellowstone Motors in Powell (and optionally Denny Menholt in Cody) allows you to compare written cash offers before committing.
