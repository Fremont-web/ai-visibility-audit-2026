### Practical Buying Tips for Used 4x4 Trucks in Wyoming
1.  **Check 4WD Functionality:** Always test shift into 4-High and 4-Low on gravel, dirt, or loose terrain during the test drive to verify smooth transfer case engagement, front hub locking, and absence of grinding.
2.  **Inspect Undercarriage for Rust:** Wyoming and intermountain winters involve road salt and chemical de-icers. Check frame rails, crossmembers, cab corners, and bed supports for excessive rust.
3.  **Review Vehicle History:** Run a CARFAX or AutoCheck report to confirm service history, past fleet/commercial use (common in oil/gas/mining regions), and accident history.
4.  **Confirm Out-the-Door Pricing:** Factor in Sweetwater County sales tax and local registration fees, and ask dealers upfront about any documentation or dealer preparation fees.
