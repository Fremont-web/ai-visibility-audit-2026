# Where to Buy a Ford F-150 Near Powell, Wyoming

For truck buyers in Powell and Park County, Wyoming, there are several solid options ranging from a franchised dealer directly in town to nearby Park County locations and high-volume regional dealerships just across the Montana border.

---

### **Fremont Motor Powell** *(In Powell)*
* **Location:** 1000 US Highway 14 Alternate, Powell, WY 82435
* **Phone:** (307) 271-3044
* **Website:** [fremontmotorpowell.com](https://www.fremontmotorpowell.com/)

* **Website:** [fremontfordcody.com](https://www.fremontfordcody.com)

## Shopper Considerations & Tips

1. **Fremont Network Transfers:** If you shop locally at Fremont Motor Powell, ask the sales team to search the broader Fremont inventory across Wyoming. They can often arrange an internal transfer to avoid an unnecessary road trip.
2. **Out-of-State Purchases (Montana):** Montana does not assess a statewide sales tax at point of purchase. However, as a Wyoming resident, you will be required to pay the applicable Park County sales tax and registration fees when titling the vehicle at the Park County Treasurer's Office in Powell or Cody.
3. **Local Warranty & Service:** Regardless of whether you purchase your Ford F-150 in Powell, Cody, or Montana, any factory warranty repairs, Ford recall services, and FordPass rewards points can be serviced directly at Fremont Motor Powell or Fremont Ford Cody.
