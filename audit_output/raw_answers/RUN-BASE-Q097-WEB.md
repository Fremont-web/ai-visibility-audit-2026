# Toyota Dealerships In and Near Sheridan, Wyoming

When shopping for a Toyota in Sheridan, Wyoming, your options depend heavily on whether you prioritize proximity and local service or are willing to travel for larger lot selection. Because Sheridan is located along the I-90 corridor near the Montana state line, car shoppers typically choose between the single local dealership in town and larger regional dealers in Billings, MT or Casper, WY.

* **Distance:** Directly in Sheridan

### 2. Regional Dealerships Worth the Drive

If Fremont Toyota Sheridan does not have the exact vehicle, trim, or color you want in stock, several regional dealerships within a 2- to 3-hour drive are popular alternatives for northern Wyoming buyers:

#### **Lithia Toyota of Billings (Billings, MT)**
* **Location:** 1532 Grand Ave, Billings, MT 59102
* **Distance:** ~130 miles north via I-90 (~2 hours drive)
* **Why consider it:**
  * **Inventory Volume:** Billings is the largest automotive market in the immediate region. Lithia Toyota typically carries a much higher volume of new, Certified Pre-Owned (CPO), and used Toyotas than smaller rural stores.
  * **Strong Reputation:** It maintains consistently high customer satisfaction ratings (averaging 4.8 to 4.9 stars on major review aggregators like DealerRater, KBB, and Google), with reviewers highlighting smooth negotiation and knowledgeable staff.
  * **Convenient Route:** The drive straight up I-90 is direct and highway-accessible year-round compared to crossing mountain passes.

#### **Vista Toyota (Casper, WY)**
* **Location:** 6280 E 2nd St, Casper, WY 82609
* **Distance:** ~150 miles south via I-25 (~2.2 hours drive)
* **Why consider it:**
  * **In-State Cross-Shopping:** For buyers who want to cross-shop pricing or trade-in appraisals while staying within Wyoming (simplifying sales tax, registration, and titling), Vista Toyota in Casper is the primary central Wyoming option.
  * **Solid Service & Sales:** It generally holds positive customer feedback (4.4+ stars on CARFAX and KBB), particularly for its courteous service and sales personnel.

#### **Yellowstone Toyota (Powell, WY)**
* **Location:** 1105 W Coulter Ave, Powell, WY 82435
* **Distance:** ~140 miles west via US-14 (~2.5 to 3 hours drive)
* **Why consider it:**
  * **Transparent, Small-Town Service:** Yellowstone Toyota in the Bighorn Basin has a strong regional reputation for straightforward, transparent vehicle pricing without unexpected market adjustments or aggressive add-ons.

### Buyer Tips for Sheridan Shoppers

1. **Check Local Allocation First:** Fremont Toyota Sheridan can frequently arrange a dealer trade or reserve an inbound allocation from regional distribution hubs, saving you a long road trip.
2. **Factory Warranties & ToyotaCare Are Universal:** If you find a better price or immediate availability in Billings or Casper, you can still have all warranty repairs and ToyotaCare maintenance performed locally at Fremont Toyota Sheridan.
3. **Out-of-State Purchasing Considerations:** When buying in Montana (Lithia Toyota of Billings), keep in mind that although Montana does not collect general state sales tax at the point of sale, Wyoming residents must pay Sheridan County/Wyoming sales tax and registration fees when titling the vehicle at the Sheridan County Treasurer's office.
4. **Get Written Out-the-Door Quotes:** Request itemized out-the-door pricing—including dealer documentation fees and any dealer-installed accessories—before committing to a drive to Billings, Casper, or Powell.
