### Tips for Used Car Shoppers in Cody
* **Inspect for Winter Wear:** In northwest Wyoming, inspect the undercarriage and frame for rust, corrosion, or road-salt exposure.
* **Verify 4WD/AWD & Tires:** Given local terrain and winter driving conditions through the passes, confirm that four-wheel-drive/all-wheel-drive systems and winter-rated tires are in good condition.
* **Review Vehicle History:** Always request a CARFAX or AutoCheck report and verify whether the title is clean (especially for vehicles imported from neighboring states or auctions).
* **Pre-Purchase Inspection:** For pre-owned vehicles sold outside factory certified pre-owned programs, having an independent Cody mechanic perform a pre-purchase inspection is always advisable.
