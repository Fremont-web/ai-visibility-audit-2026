If you are looking to purchase a reliable used Ford backed by a warranty in or near Sheridan, Wyoming, you have both local and regional options, ranging from factory-backed Ford Certified Pre-Owned (CPO) programs to dealer-specific limited warranties.

### 1. Primary Local Option: Fremont Ford Sheridan
* **Location:** 1658 Coffeen Ave, Sheridan, WY 82801
* **Overview:** As Sheridan's authorized Ford franchise dealership, Fremont Ford Sheridan is the primary local destination for Ford-backed certified used vehicles.
* **Warranty Programs Available:**
  * **Ford Blue Advantage (Gold Certified):** Ford vehicles under 6 years old with fewer than 80,000 miles that pass a 172-point inspection come with a 12-month/12,000-mile comprehensive limited warranty and a 7-year/100,000-mile powertrain limited warranty from original in-service date, plus 24/7 roadside assistance.
  * **Ford Blue Advantage (Blue Certified):** Used vehicles up to 10 years old with under 120,000–150,000 miles that complete a 139-point inspection include a 90-day/4,000-mile comprehensive warranty.
  * **Fremont Elite / Fremont Certified:** For inventory not enrolled in the factory CPO program, Fremont offers store-level coverage, such as Fremont Elite (103-point inspection with a 12-month/12,000-mile limited warranty) or Fremont Certified (70-point inspection with a 3-month/3,000-mile warranty).
  * **Fremont Care:** Eligible used vehicle purchases often include 1 year of complimentary maintenance (two oil changes and two tire rotations).

### Buyer Recommendations & Tips
1. **Verify the Warranty Tier:** Ask whether the Ford is covered under **Ford Blue Advantage Gold**, **Ford Blue Advantage Blue**, or an **aftermarket/dealer warranty**. Gold Certified provides the strongest peace of mind with 7-year/100,000-mile powertrain coverage.
2. **Review Vehicle History:** Always examine the included CARFAX or AutoCheck report to confirm maintenance history, ownership records, and accident reports.
3. **Confirm Where Work Can Be Performed:** Manufacturer-backed CPO warranties can be honored at any Ford dealership in the country, whereas certain dealer-specific warranties may require servicing at the originating dealership group.
