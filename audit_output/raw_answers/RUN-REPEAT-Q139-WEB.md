### Buyer Tips for Used Dodge Vehicles with Warranties

1. **Verify CPO vs. Service Contract:** Factory Stellantis CPO warranties are honored at any authorized CDJR dealership nationwide with standardized deductibles (typically $100). Third-party service contracts vary widely in terms of covered components, labor rate limits, and approved repair facilities.
2. **Check Remaining Factory Warranty:** Recent-model Dodge vehicles may still have balance remaining on the original 3-year/36,000-mile basic or 5-year/60,000-mile powertrain factory warranty, which automatically transfers to subsequent owners.
3. **Request Vehicle History & Inspection Reports:** Always review the CARFAX or AutoCheck report and request the technician inspection checklist before finalizing the purchase.
