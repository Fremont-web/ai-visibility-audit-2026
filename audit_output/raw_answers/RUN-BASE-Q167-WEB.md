### Important Shopper Tips for Used Rams with Warranties

1. **Confirm the Warranty Type:** A factory **Mopar/Ram CPO warranty** is honored at any CDJR dealership across North America. A dealer-backed warranty (like Fremont Elite or an aftermarket vehicle service contract) may have specific terms, networks, or deductibles—always verify whether repairs can be completed at outside shops.
2. **Check Remaining Factory Warranty:** If looking at a 2- to 4-year-old truck, check the VIN for the original "in-service date." Ram trucks come standard from the factory with a 5-year/60,000-mile powertrain warranty on gas engines (5.7L Hemi / 3.6L Pentastar) and 5-year/100,000-mile powertrain coverage on 6.7L Cummins turbo diesels, which automatically transfers to subsequent owners.
3. **Request Vehicle History & Maintenance Records:** Especially in Wyoming, inspect the undercarriage for salt and gravel wear, and review maintenance history for timely transmission fluid changes and exhaust manifold checks (a known wear point on Hemi V8s).
