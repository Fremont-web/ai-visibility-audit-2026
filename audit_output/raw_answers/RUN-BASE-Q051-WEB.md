### Key Tips for Used Chrysler Buyers
1. **Verify the Warranty Type:** Confirm in writing whether the car is **Factory CDJR CPO** (valid at any Chrysler dealer nationwide), **Dealer Certified** (e.g., Fremont Certified), or an **aftermarket third-party policy**.
2. **Inspect Chrysler Pacifica Specifics:** If you are buying a Pacifica or Pacifica Plug-in Hybrid (PHEV), check that the battery and hybrid electrical components retain their factory warranty or are explicitly included under the pre-owned coverage.
3. **Review Vehicle History:** Always obtain a full CARFAX or AutoCheck report to verify regular servicing and ensure all open safety recalls have been resolved.
