* **Call Ahead with Your VIN:** Before taking your vehicle in, check your 17-digit Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) on the official [Ford Recall Portal](https://www.ford.com/support/how-tos/recall/recalls-and-faqs/how-can-i-confirm-if-my-vehicle-has-a-recall/) or [NHTSA.gov](https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls). When scheduling, provide your VIN to the service department so they can confirm that the specific recall remedy parts are physically in stock before your appointment.
