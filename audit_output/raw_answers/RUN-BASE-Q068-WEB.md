### Tips for Selling Directly to a Dealership
1. **Bring Necessary Paperwork:** Carry your vehicle title (or loan account details/payoff letter if financed), current vehicle registration, a government-issued photo ID, and all keys/key fobs.
2. **Get an Independent Baseline:** Before heading to the dealership, obtain a baseline valuation using independent tools like the Kelley Blue Book (KBB) Instant Cash Offer or Edmunds appraisal so you can evaluate the dealer's cash offer competitively.
3. **Expect an In-Person Inspection:** Online estimates are contingent upon the dealer's physical check of cosmetic condition, mechanical health, tire tread, and vehicle history (accident/service records).
