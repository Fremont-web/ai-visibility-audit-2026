### Key Wyoming Factors to Understand Before Requesting Your Quote

1.  **No Cap on Documentation Fees:** Wyoming state law does not cap dealer documentation fees. In the Sheridan region, doc fees typically run from **$299 to $599** (statewide average is ~$460). While dealers generally refuse to delete the doc fee line item (to avoid fair-lending compliance issues), the total OTD price is negotiable—you can ask for a vehicle price discount equivalent to the doc fee.
2.  **Wyoming Vehicle Sales Tax Collection:** In Wyoming, vehicle sales tax is assessed based on the buyer's county of residence (**Sheridan County is currently 6%**), not where the vehicle is bought. By state law, sales tax is paid to your **County Treasurer within 65 days** of purchase. When paying cash or arranging outside financing, dealerships often provide an OTD worksheet that shows vehicle price + doc fee, noting that taxes are payable at the treasurer's office. If you are financing through the dealer, they can roll the exact Sheridan County tax into your loan and disburse it to the county.
3.  **Watch for Mandatory Add-Ons:** Always check the written quote for pre-installed items such as ceramic coatings, interior protection packages, window tint, or anti-theft etching. Request that any unwanted add-ons be removed or discounted completely.
