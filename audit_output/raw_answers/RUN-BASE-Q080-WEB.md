### Practical Steps Before Scheduling

1. **Verify Open Recalls:** Look up active campaigns using your 17-character Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) on the [Mopar Recall Lookup](https://www.mopar.com/en-us/my-vehicle/recalls/search.html) or the [NHTSA Recalls Tool](https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls).
2. **Call Ahead with Your VIN:** Recall remedies often require specific parts kits. Calling the service department in advance allows them to verify inventory and order any necessary parts before you bring your vehicle in.
3. **Warranty Claims:** If seeking warranty repairs for mechanical or electrical issues, ensure factory coverage terms are active and inform the service advisor prior to diagnostic work.
