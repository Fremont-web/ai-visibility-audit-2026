### Key Considerations for a Casper Buyer

1. **Range & Wyoming Climate:** The current Fiat 500e has an EPA-estimated range of roughly 149 miles per charge. In Wyoming's winter temperatures and gusty crosswinds, real-world battery range will drop by 30% to 40%. The 500e is an excellent runabout for daily commuting around Casper, Evansville, and Mills, but is not designed for non-stop cross-state travel without planned charging stops.
2. **Out-of-State Purchasing & Incentives:** If buying from Denver, verify how incentives apply. Some advertised Colorado lease specials rely on Colorado state resident tax credits; as a Wyoming resident, you will qualify for federal tax credits or manufacturer lease pass-through incentives, but not Colorado state-specific rebates.
3. **Charging Infrastructure:** Setting up a dedicated 240V Level 2 charging outlet at home is strongly advised, as DC fast-charging network density remains relatively spread out in central Wyoming.
