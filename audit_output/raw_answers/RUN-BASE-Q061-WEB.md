**Question:** What are the best Ram dealerships in or near Cody, Wyoming, and why?

**SHA-256 Hash:** `None`

---

When shopping for a Ram truck in or around Cody, Wyoming, your options range from the immediate local franchise in town to nearby Big Horn Basin neighbors and high-volume regional dealers in Montana.

Because Cody is a rural market, most truck buyers compare the convenience of the local dealership against the substantially larger selection available in Billings.

---

### 1. Fremont Chrysler Dodge Jeep Ram Cody (Cody, WY) – Best for Local Convenience & Service
* **Location:** 3127 Big Horn Ave, Cody, WY 82414
* **Distance:** Local (in town)
* **Why it's a top choice:**
  * **Sole Local Franchise:** This is the only authorized Ram dealership physically located in Cody, making it the most convenient option for sales, factory warranty repairs, Mopar parts, and routine maintenance.
  * **Network Inventory Access:** As part of Fremont Motor Companies (Wyoming's largest dealership group), the Cody location can source and transfer Ram trucks from sister stores across the state (such as Casper, Lander, Powell, or Rock Springs).
  * **Fremont Care Program:** New and pre-owned vehicle purchases include 1 year of complimentary maintenance (typically covering two oil changes and tire rotations) and roadside assistance.
* **Shopper Considerations:** Because it is a smaller rural dealership, physical on-lot inventory for specialized trims or heavy-duty configurations can be limited. Customer reviews are mixed, with praise for friendly local staff and quick emergency repairs, but occasional complaints regarding service department turnaround times.

---

### 2. Fremont Motor Powell (Powell, WY) – Best Nearby Alternative
* **Location:** 1000 US Highway 14A, Powell, WY 82435
* **Distance:** ~24 miles northeast (approx. 25–30 minutes from Cody)
* **Why it's a top choice:**
  * **Close Proximity:** Powell is just a short drive from Cody, providing Big Horn Basin residents with a quick alternative without leaving the immediate area.
  * **Dual Franchise & Service Alternative:** It operates as an authorized CDJR and Ford dealer, offering factory-certified Ram service and sales. If the Cody store lacks appointment availability or specific inventory, Powell offers a second nearby option while honoring the same Fremont Care benefits and network pricing.
* **Shopper Considerations:** Like Cody, physical on-lot inventory is modest, though vehicles can be transferred from across the Fremont group.

---

### 3. Lithia Chrysler Jeep Dodge of Billings (Billings, MT) – Best for Selection & Volume Pricing
* **Location:** 2229 King Ave W, Billings, MT 59102
* **Distance:** ~100 miles northeast (approx. 1 hour 45 minutes to 2 hours drive)
* **Why it's a top choice:**
  * **Regional Volume & Selection:** Billings is the primary commercial hub for Northern Wyoming. Lithia is a high-volume dealership with one of the largest selections of Ram 1500, 2500, and 3500 trucks in the region. If you are seeking a specific trim (such as a Rebel, Laramie, Longhorn, or Limited), a heavy-duty Cummins diesel, or commercial chassis cab configurations, Lithia is far more likely to have multiple matching units in stock.
  * **Competitive Pricing:** Higher sales volumes often give larger metro stores more pricing flexibility and aggressive manufacturer-rebate utilization.
  * **Strong Customer Feedback:** Highly rated across major consumer automotive platforms (averaging ~4.5/5 on Cars.com and 4.4/5 on CARFAX across hundreds of customer reviews).
* **Shopper Considerations:** The 100-mile drive makes routine oil changes and warranty visits less practical unless combined with other trips to Billings. Note that while Montana does not have a state sales tax, Wyoming buyers must pay Park County/Wyoming sales tax and registration when titling the vehicle back home in Wyoming.

---

### 4. Sheridan Motors CDJR (Sheridan, WY) – Regional In-State Competitor
* **Location:** 3270 Wrench Dr, Sheridan, WY 82801
* **Distance:** ~145–165 miles east (approx. 2.5 to 3 hours drive)
* **Why it's worth knowing:** If you prefer to buy within Wyoming but want to negotiate outside the Fremont Motor Companies network, Sheridan Motors CDJR is the nearest independent in-state competitor.
* **Shopper Considerations:** The drive requires crossing the Bighorn Mountains via US-14 or US-14A, which can feature hazardous weather, seasonal closures, or difficult winter driving conditions.

---

### Practical Recommendations for Cody Buyers
1. **Start Local:** Check Fremont CDJR Cody first. If they don't have the exact Ram 1500 or 2500/3500 configuration you want on the lot, ask if they can transfer one from another Fremont location in Wyoming.
2. **Cross-Shop Billings for Heavy-Duty or Rare Trims:** If you need a specialized work truck, high-end trim, or immediate selection, check Lithia CDJR in Billings before making a final deal.
3. **Warranty is Universal:** Any authorized Ram dealership is obligated to perform warranty work and recall services regardless of where the truck was originally purchased. You can buy in Billings to get the best inventory match or price, then have routine warranty servicing completed locally in Cody or Powell.
