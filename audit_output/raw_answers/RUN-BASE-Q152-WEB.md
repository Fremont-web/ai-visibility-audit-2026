### **Tips for Selling Outright to a Dealership**
1.  **Bring Required Paperwork:** Have your clean title (or lienholder account information and 10-day payoff statement if financed), current vehicle registration, and valid government ID.
2.  **Bring All Accessories:** Extra keys, fobs, owner's manuals, and wheel lock keys can increase or protect the final offer amount.
3.  **Get Multiple Appraisals:** Dealership offers can vary significantly based on their current inventory needs. Compare an online baseline offer (such as SellYourCarWyoming.com or KBB Instant Cash Offer) against at least two local dealer bids before accepting an offer.
