### 3. Understanding the Types of Warranties on Used Chryslers

When buying a used Chrysler with warranty protection, clarify which type of coverage is included:

1. **Manufacturer Certified Pre-Owned (Stellantis CPO):** 
   * Factory-backed coverage accepted at any authorized Chrysler/Dodge/Jeep/Ram dealer nationwide.
   * Includes up to a 7-year/100,000-mile powertrain warranty and a 3-month/3,000-mile Maximum Care limited warranty.
2. **Dealer-Branded Limited Warranty (e.g., Fremont Care):**
   * Backed by the dealer network rather than the manufacturer.
   * Typically provides 12-month/12,000-mile or 3-month/3,000-mile mechanical coverage and complimentary maintenance perks.
3. **Extended Service Contracts (Mopar Vehicle Protection):**
   * Optional comprehensive vehicle service contracts that can be added at the time of sale to extend mechanical and electrical coverage past factory terms.
