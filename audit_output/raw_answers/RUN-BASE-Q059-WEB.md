### Practical Tips for Buying a Used Jeep with a Warranty
1. **Verify Factory vs. Aftermarket Warranty:** Always ask whether a listed warranty is backed directly by Mopar/Stellantis (usable at any authorized CDJR dealer nationwide) or is an aftermarket service contract administered by a third party (which may require specific repair shops or deductibles).
2. **Check Remaining Original Warranty:** Many late-model used Jeeps (under 3 years old or under 36,000 miles) carry the remainder of the original 3-year/36,000-mile bumper-to-bumper and 5-year/60,000-mile powertrain warranties, which transfer automatically to subsequent owners.
3. **Request History & Maintenance Records:** Jeeps are frequently used for towing, off-roading, or winter driving in Wyoming. Ask for the vehicle history report (CARFAX/AutoCheck) and confirm regular service, 4x4 system maintenance, and no frame/rust issues.
4. **Get an Independent Pre-Purchase Inspection (PPI):** If buying from an independent lot or non-certified dealer, have a qualified independent mechanic inspect the 4WD components, suspension, and steering prior to finalizing your contract.
