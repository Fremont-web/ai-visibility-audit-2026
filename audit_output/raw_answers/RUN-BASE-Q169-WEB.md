### Used Car Dealerships in Rawlins, Wyoming

Because Rawlins is a relatively small community in Carbon County, its local automotive retail market is concentrated. Directly within Rawlins city limits, there are two primary franchised dealerships that maintain substantial pre-owned vehicle inventories. Both dealerships are situated along East Cedar Street.

---

### Local Dealerships in Rawlins

#### 1. Fremont Motor Rawlins (Chrysler, Dodge, Jeep, Ram)
* **Location:** 823 E Cedar St, Rawlins, WY 82301
* **Inventory & Focus:** Part of the statewide Fremont Motor Companies dealer group, this store carries certified pre-owned (CPO) Chrysler, Dodge, Jeep, and Ram models, as well as a variety of multi-brand used cars, pickup trucks, and SUVs. They routinely provide vehicle history reports (such as CARFAX) and pre-owned vehicle warranties through programs like Fremont Care.
* **Key Advantage:** Because they belong to a large Wyoming dealership network, shoppers can browse inventory at sister lots across the state (including Rock Springs, Casper, Lander, and Riverton) and often arrange for vehicles to be transferred to Rawlins for viewing and purchase.

#### 2. Skyline Motors (Chevrolet, GMC)
* **Location:** 911 E Cedar St, Rawlins, WY 82301
* **Inventory & Focus:** Skyline Motors is a long-standing local franchise dealer carrying General Motors brands (Chevrolet and GMC) alongside an inventory of pre-owned vehicles from various domestic and foreign manufacturers. They feature GM Certified Pre-Owned and CarBravo certified pre-owned selections, as well as budget-conscious trade-ins priced under $15,000.
* **Key Advantage:** Established in-town service and parts department with deep local roots, providing convenient local warranty service and maintenance for GM and pre-owned vehicles.

### Regional Alternatives

Because Rawlins does not have local franchise dealerships for popular brands such as Ford, Toyota, Honda, or Subaru, many area buyers expand their search to neighboring automotive hubs within a 1- to 2-hour drive along Interstate 80 or US-287:

* **Rock Springs, WY (~105 miles west on I-80):** Offers a larger volume of truck and import dealerships, including:
  * **Wyoming Trucks:** A large regional pre-owned dealership specializing heavily in pickup trucks and SUVs.
  * **Rock Springs Honda Toyota:** For certified and pre-owned import vehicles.
  * **First Choice Ford:** Regional franchise Ford inventory and pre-owned trucks.
  * **Whisler Chevrolet:** Additional GM pre-owned options.

* **Laramie, WY (~100 miles east on I-80):** A university community with a steady flow of pre-owned vehicles and trade-ins, including:
  * **Laramie Range Ford:** Franchise Ford inventory and used vehicles.
  * **Laramie Auto Center:** Franchise Chevrolet/GMC pre-owned inventory.
  * **Johnson Auto of Laramie:** CDJR and multi-brand used inventory.
  * **Alpine Motors LLC:** A local independent used vehicle lot specializing in budget-friendly pre-owned cars, trucks, and SUVs.

* **Casper, WY (~115 miles north via US-287 / WY-220) & Cheyenne, WY (~145 miles east on I-80):** Wyoming's largest vehicle retail hubs with extensive franchise selections, dedicated pre-owned lots, and high vehicle turnover.

### Buyer Tips for Rawlins Shoppers

1. **Check Online Inventory Frequently:** Local lots in Rawlins have smaller physical footprints than regional superstores. Inventory changes rapidly with local trade-ins, so browsing websites before visiting is recommended.
2. **Inquire About Dealer Group Transfers:** If looking at Fremont Motor Rawlins, ask whether a vehicle located at another Fremont dealership in Wyoming can be transferred locally.
3. **Inspect for Winter Readiness:** Wyoming driving requires dependable cold-weather capability. Verify 4WD/AWD system functionality, battery health, cabin heat and defrost systems, tire tread depth, and undercarriage condition for road-treatment corrosion.
4. **Verify Vehicle History & Pre-Purchase Inspections:** Request a complete CARFAX or AutoCheck report and consider an independent mechanical inspection prior to finalizing a used purchase.
