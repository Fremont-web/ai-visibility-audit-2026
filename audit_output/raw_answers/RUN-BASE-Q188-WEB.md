### 3. Essential Advice for Fiat Recall & Warranty Repairs

1. **Safety Recalls Are Free of Charge:** Under federal regulation, all safety recall repairs must be completed at zero cost to the vehicle owner by an authorized manufacturer dealership, regardless of vehicle age, mileage, or warranty status.
2. **Verify Open Recalls Online Before You Call:**
   * **Mopar Recall Lookup:** Visit [mopar.com/recalls](https://www.mopar.com) or [fiatusa.com/webselfservice/fiatusa/recall](https://www.fiatusa.com).
   * **NHTSA Recall Database:** Check the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration site at [nhtsa.gov/recalls](https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls).
   * Have your **17-digit Vehicle Identification Number (VIN)** ready (found on your dashboard or registration).
3. **Always Call Ahead with Your VIN:** Fiat models utilize distinct powertrains, parts, and software relative to standard CDJR vehicles. Always phone the service department before your visit with your VIN to confirm:
   * That they currently have a certified Fiat technician on staff.
   * That specific recall replacement parts are in stock or ordered ahead of your appointment.
   * For electric models (**Fiat 500e**), confirm whether the facility is certified and equipped with the required high-voltage EV diagnostic tools.
