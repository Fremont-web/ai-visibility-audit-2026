### Shopper Recommendations & Checklist
1. **Confirm Certification Level:** When browsing used Fords at a Ford dealer, verify whether the vehicle is **Gold Certified** (manufacturer 12 mo/12k mi comprehensive + 7 yr/100k mi powertrain), **Blue Certified** (90-day comprehensive), **Dealer Certified** (3-month or 12-month Fremont warranty), or sold **As-Is**.
2. **Check Remaining Factory Warranty:** If the Ford is less than 3 years old or under 36,000 miles (or under 5 years/60,000 miles for powertrain), the original Ford factory warranty may still be active and will transfer to you.
3. **Review Vehicle History:** Always review the complimentary CARFAX or AutoCheck report provided by certified dealers to confirm maintenance records and ensure there is no prior frame damage, salvage title, or unaddressed recall.
4. **Clarify Deductibles & Service Locations:** Ford Blue Advantage warranties feature a $100 deductible and are honored at any authorized Ford dealership nationwide.
