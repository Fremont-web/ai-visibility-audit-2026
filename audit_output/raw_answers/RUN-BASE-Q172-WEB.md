### Practical Tips When Selling Without Buying

1. **Title and Payoff Information:** If you own the vehicle free and clear, bring the physical title in your name and a government-issued photo ID. If you still have an active auto loan, bring your lender information, account number, and 10-day payoff balance—dealerships can pay off your lender directly and issue you a check for any positive equity.
2. **Valuation Expectations:** Dealerships buy at wholesale market value to account for reconditioning and resale margin. If you want maximum dollar value, a private sale may yield more, but selling directly to a dealership is faster, avoids stranger test-drives, and eliminates payment fraud risks.
3. **Sales Tax Note:** Remember that selling outright does not provide the Wyoming vehicle sales tax credit that a standard trade-in against a new purchase offers.
4. **Get an Online Appraisal First:** Both Fremont Motors (via SellYourCarWyoming.com) and Laramie GM offer online evaluation tools so you can obtain an estimated baseline before driving over.
