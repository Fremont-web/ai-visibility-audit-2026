### Essential Steps Before Visiting

1. **Call Ahead with Your 17-Digit VIN:** 
   While Fremont CDJR Casper carries Mopar OEM parts and provides certified maintenance for Fiat models (such as the 500, 500X, 500L, and 124 Spider), factory warranty policies require specific technician certifications and specialized tools. Call the service desk before booking to confirm that a certified technician is available for your specific warranty issue.
2. **Check Parts Availability for Recalls:** 
   By federal law, safety recall repairs are performed **100% free of charge** at authorized franchise dealerships. However, replacement recall kits (such as software flashes, airbag inflators, or fuel system parts) are frequently shipped on demand rather than kept on local shelves. Providing your Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) in advance ensures they order the parts before you drop off your vehicle.
3. **Lookup Active Recalls Online:** 
   You can verify open recalls at any time using your VIN on the official [NHTSA Recalls Tool](https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls) or the [Mopar Owner Recall Portal](https://www.mopar.com).
