### Shopper Guidance & Caveats

1. **Verify Certification Level:** Not every used Ford on a dealer's lot carries warranty coverage. Review the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) Buyer's Guide in the window to distinguish between factory CPO (**Ford Blue Advantage**), dealer-backed warranties (**Fremont Elite / Certified**), and vehicles sold strictly **"As-Is"** with only optional service contract availability.
2. **Review Vehicle History:** Always request the CARFAX or AutoCheck report (typically provided free by franchised dealers) to verify maintenance history, ownership records, and ensure there is no prior frame damage, salvage title, or flood history.
3. **Independent Pre-Purchase Inspection (PPI):** Even for warrantied vehicles, having an independent mechanic inspect the chassis, suspension, 4x4 engagement, and brake systems before final delivery is a sound practice.
4. **Warranty Servicing Logistics:** Manufacturer-backed Ford Blue Advantage warranties can be honored at any authorized Ford dealership in North America (including Fremont Ford Lander), whereas proprietary dealer warranties may have specific repair facility rules. Clarify where warranty work must be performed prior to signing.
