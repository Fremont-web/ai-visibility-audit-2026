### Important Steps Before Your Visit:
1. **Look Up Active Recalls by VIN:** Check your vehicle's 17-digit Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) on the official [Mopar Recall Tool](https://www.mopar.com/en-us/care/safety/recalls.html) or the [NHTSA Recall Search](https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls). This identifies open safety campaigns and required remedy parts.
2. **Call Ahead with Your VIN:** Authorized dealerships must often order recall-specific parts tied to your VIN before your appointment. Contact the service department ahead of time to verify part availability so you do not make an unnecessary trip.
3. **No-Cost Recall Coverage:** By federal law, safety recall repairs are performed free of charge at authorized franchised dealerships.
4. **Warranty Documentation:** For warranty claims, bring your vehicle's registration and proof of routine maintenance (oil changes, scheduled servicing) in case Stellantis requires service history for coverage approval.
