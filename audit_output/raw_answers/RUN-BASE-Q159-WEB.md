## 3. Understanding Used Dodge Warranty Types

When buying a used Dodge with warranty coverage, make sure you know which category of protection you are receiving:

1. **Stellantis Factory Certified Pre-Owned (CPO):**
   * **Powertrain Coverage:** 7 years / 100,000 miles from the original in-service date.
   * **Maximum Care (Bumper-to-Bumper) Coverage:** 3 months / 3,000 miles (or remaining balance of original factory warranty).
   * **Requirements:** 125-point inspection, clean title/CARFAX history, and 24/7 roadside assistance.
2. **Dealer-Certified Warranties (e.g., Fremont Certified/Elite):**
   * Provides 3-month/3,000-mile or 12-month/12,000-mile limited warranties backed by the dealership group on vehicles that pass multi-point inspections.
3. **Vehicle Service Contracts (Extended Warranties / Mopar Vehicle Protection):**
   * Purchased through the dealer's finance department to cover mechanical and electrical components on vehicles outside original or CPO warranty limits.
