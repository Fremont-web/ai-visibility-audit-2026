### Key Tips Before You Buy

1. **Check the Window Buyers Guide:** Under federal law, every used vehicle on a dealer's lot must display an FTC Buyers Guide indicating whether the vehicle is sold "As-Is" or with a warranty. Ensure the warranty box is checked and the specific terms are stated.
2. **Distinguish Factory CPO from Dealer Warranties:** Verify whether the vehicle is covered by an official Mopar/Chrysler CPOV warranty (accepted at any CDJR dealer nationwide) or a dealer-specific warranty/aftermarket service contract.
3. **Review Coverage Specifics & Deductibles:** Ask for written documentation detailing what components are covered (powertrain only vs. bumper-to-bumper exclusionary), the warranty deductible (typically $0 to $100 per visit), and expiration dates or mileage limits.
4. **Get a Pre-Purchase Inspection & History Report:** Always review the CARFAX or AutoCheck report to verify regular maintenance and ensure the title is clean.
