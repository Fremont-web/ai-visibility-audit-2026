**Question:** What are the best used car dealerships in Powell, Wyoming?

Because Powell is a smaller community in Wyoming's Bighorn Basin, its dedicated in-town dealership market is compact. The best dealership for you will depend primarily on whether you are looking for late-model certified pre-owned (CPO) vehicles, diverse franchise trade-ins, or flexible independent financing.

Here is a breakdown of the primary used car dealers located directly in Powell, along with notable regional alternatives nearby.

---

### Primary Used Car Dealerships in Powell, WY

#### 1. Yellowstone Motors (Yellowstone Toyota / Yellowstone Chevrolet GMC)
* **Location:** 1105 W Coulter Ave, Powell, WY 82435
* **Overview:** Yellowstone Motors is Powell's largest multi-franchise dealer campus, encompassing Toyota, Chevrolet, and GMC franchises. 
* **Used Inventory Strengths:** They maintain the area's broadest inventory of late-model used trucks, SUVs, and passenger vehicles. Because they carry both GM and Toyota franchises, shoppers can access manufacturer-backed Certified Pre-Owned (CPO) vehicles, which come with rigorous inspections and extended factory warranties.
* **Consumer Notes:** Customer reviews frequently highlight courteous, low-pressure sales interactions and dependable service department support.

#### 2. Fremont Motor Powell
* **Location:** 1000 US Highway 14A, Powell, WY 82435
* **Overview:** Part of the statewide Fremont Motor Companies group, this location is Powell's local dealership for Ford, Chrysler, Dodge, Jeep, and RAM vehicles.
* **Used Inventory Strengths:** They offer a substantial selection of pre-owned domestic trucks and SUVs, including "Fremont Certified" vehicles backed by multi-point inspections and dealer-group warranty coverage. Being part of a larger dealer group also means they can occasionally facilitate inventory transfers from other Fremont locations across Wyoming.
* **Consumer Notes:** While service interactions are generally rated favorably, buyers should ask for clear, itemized out-the-door pricing in writing during negotiation.

#### 3. Dorm's Auto (Dorm's Sales & Service)
* **Location:** 842 E South St, Powell, WY 82435
* **Overview:** A long-standing, family-owned independent dealership and repair facility operating in Powell since 1976.
* **Used Inventory Strengths:** Dorm's specializes in affordable, budget-conscious pre-owned vehicles. They offer in-house "Buy Here Pay Here" (BHPH) financing, making them a practical local option for shoppers rebuilding credit or needing flexible terms.
* **Consumer Notes:** They also run an ASE-certified repair shop on site, providing ongoing maintenance and service.

* **Denny Menholt Cody Chevrolet (Cody):** Offers additional regional pre-owned GM inventory and trade-ins.

### Shopper Tips for the Bighorn Basin

1. **Verify Vehicle History:** Always request a CARFAX or AutoCheck report to check for past winter-road rust, multi-owner history, or salvage/rebuilt branding.
2. **Get an Independent Pre-Purchase Inspection (PPI):** Even if a vehicle has been dealer-inspected, an independent mechanical inspection provides added protection, especially for older or off-warranty trucks and 4WD vehicles.
3. **Confirm Total Pricing:** Clarify all documentation fees, titling costs, and regional registration fees upfront to ensure full transparency before signing.
