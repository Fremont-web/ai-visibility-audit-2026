### Important Shopper Tips for Recall & Warranty Repairs

* **Recalls Are Free:** By law, safety recall repairs are completed 100% free of charge for parts and labor at any authorized franchise dealership.
* **Confirm Open Recalls Online:** You can verify open recalls prior to your appointment by entering your 17-digit Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) on [Mopar's Recall Lookup](https://www.mopar.com/en-us/my-vehicle/recalls.html) or the [NHTSA Recalls Portal](https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls).
* **Call Ahead with Your VIN:** Dealerships frequently need to pre-order specific recall remedy kits or warranty components before your arrival. Providing your VIN when booking ensures the necessary parts are physically in stock before you drop off your vehicle.
