### Practical Advice Before Scheduling Recall or Warranty Service

1. **Check for Open Recalls Online First:** You can verify open recalls free of charge by entering your 17-digit Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) on either the [Official Mopar Safety Recalls Lookup](https://www.mopar.com/en-us/care/safety/recalls.html) or the federal database at [NHTSA.gov/recalls](https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls).
2. **Recall Repairs Are 100% Free:** Safety recall remedies performed by an authorized CDJR dealer are provided at no charge to vehicle owners.
3. **Call Ahead with Your VIN:** Even if you book online, contact the service department to confirm that the required recall remedy parts are in stock. Dealerships frequently need to order specific replacement components prior to your service visit.
4. **Warranty Coverage Requirements:** For repairs under the factory bumper-to-bumper or powertrain warranty, make sure to bring your vehicle maintenance records in case proof of regular maintenance is requested.
