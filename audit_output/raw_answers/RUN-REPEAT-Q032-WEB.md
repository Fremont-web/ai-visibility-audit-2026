### Practical Advice for Shoppers & Vehicle Owners

1. **Locate Your VIN:** Have your 17-character Vehicle Identification Number (found on your dashboard, driver's door jamb, or registration card) ready when scheduling. Service advisors use it to look up active field service actions and your warranty eligibility.
2. **Recall Costs:** Manufacturer safety recall repairs are performed free of charge to vehicle owners at authorized Ford dealerships, regardless of where or from whom you purchased the car.
3. **Call Ahead for Parts:** Some recall remedies require specific replacement kits or specialized software updates. Contact the service department beforehand to verify that all necessary parts are on-site before your visit.
