### 3. Critical Factors to Know About Out-the-Door Pricing in Wyoming

When reviewing a written OTD quote in Casper, keep these state-specific nuances in mind:

1. **Wyoming Sales Tax Collection (Natrona County):**
   * The combined state and local sales tax in Natrona County is **5%** (4% state + 1% optional county tax).
   * In Wyoming, dealerships **do not routinely collect sales tax** on cash purchases or outside-lender purchases. Instead, Wyoming law allows consumers up to **64 days** from the purchase date to pay the sales/use tax directly to the County Treasurer's office when titling and registering the vehicle. 
   * If you are financing through the dealership, they can roll the 5% tax into your loan amount on the written contract. Always clarify with the dealer whether the tax is included in the check/loan amount or if you will be paying it directly at the Natrona County Courthouse.
2. **No Legal Cap on Documentation Fees:**
   * Wyoming state law does not limit dealer documentation fees. In Casper, doc fees range from roughly $349 up to $599. While dealers rarely waive the fee line-item (to maintain regulatory consistency), you can negotiate a discount on the vehicle's selling price to offset higher fees.
3. **Watch for Mandatory Dealer Add-Ons:**
   * Always verify whether dealer add-ons (such as paint protection, tint, door edge guards, or anti-theft etching) are included in the quote. A legitimate out-the-door breakdown must clearly itemize or remove these if you do not want them.
