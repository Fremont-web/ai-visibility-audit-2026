### Key Tips When Buying a Sub-$25k 4WD Truck in Wyoming

1.  **Check for Frame Rust & Undercarriage Corrosion:** Winter highway treatments (magnesium chloride and road salt) can corrode frame rails, crossmembers, cab corners, and brake lines. Always inspect underneath the vehicle.
2.  **Verify 4WD Functionality:** Engage 4WD High and 4WD Low on dirt or gravel to ensure the transfer case, front actuator, and differentials operate smoothly without grinding or delay.
3.  **Review the Title & History:** Request a CARFAX or AutoCheck report to check for prior accidents, salvage/rebuilt titles, or heavy commercial/fleet service.
4.  **Independent Pre-Purchase Inspection:** Before finalizing a purchase, have a trusted local mechanic in Powell or Cody inspect the vehicle's engine, transmission, steering linkage, and suspension.
