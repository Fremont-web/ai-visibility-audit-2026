### Important Shopper & Service Tips for Recalls & Warranty Work

1. **Recalls Are Free:** By law, authorized dealerships must perform safety and emissions recall repairs at zero cost to the owner, regardless of whether your vehicle is under warranty or purchased used.
2. **Check Open Recalls with Your VIN:** Check your 17-digit Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) in advance on the [Official Mopar Recall Portal](https://www.mopar.com/en-us/my-vehicle/recalls.html) or [NHTSA.gov/recalls](https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls) to view open campaigns and check for urgent "Do Not Drive" notices.
3. **Call Ahead for Parts Availability:** Many recall campaigns and specific warranty components require dedicated repair kits that dealers order as needed. Call the service department with your VIN prior to your visit so they can ensure the necessary parts are on site.
4. **Warranty Documentation:** Bring your vehicle registration and maintenance logs if you have an aftermarket or factory powertrain warranty requiring proof of routine maintenance.
