**Question:** What are the best Ram dealerships in or near Powell, Wyoming, and why?

**SHA-256 Hash:** `None`

---

When shopping for a Ram truck in or around **Powell, Wyoming**, your options range from a convenient hometown dealership to regional high-volume alternatives in southern Montana and northern Wyoming.

Because Powell is a smaller community in the Big Horn Basin, dealership choices follow a clear geographical tier: you have direct local access right in town, a close sister dealership in neighboring Cody, and larger regional dealerships if you need extensive on-lot inventory.

---

### 1. Local Dealerships (Powell & Park County)

#### **Fremont Motor Powell** – *Powell, WY (Local)*
* **Location:** 1000 US Highway 14A, Powell, WY 82435
* **Distance from Powell:** In town (0 miles)
* **Why It’s a Top Choice:**
  * **Hometown Convenience:** Fremont Motor Powell is the only factory-authorized Ram dealership located directly within Powell. It also carries Chrysler, Dodge, Jeep, and Ford under one roof.
  * **Local Mopar Service & Warranty Support:** Having an authorized service and parts department right on Highway 14A makes routine maintenance, warranty work, and factory recalls hassle-free without having to make highway trips.
  * **Fremont Care Perks:** Vehicles purchased from Fremont group stores generally qualify for "Fremont Care," which provides complimentary initial maintenance perks (such as oil changes and tire rotations).
  * **Fremont Network Access:** Because it is part of the Fremont Motor Companies network—Wyoming’s largest dealership group—sales staff can often locate and transfer Ram 1500, 2500, or 3500 models from sister stores across Wyoming.
* **Things to Consider:** As a smaller rural branch, physical on-lot inventory of heavy-duty or niche trims may be limited compared to metropolitan volume stores. Pricing transparency monitors also advise calling or visiting in person to secure full out-the-door pricing.

#### **Fremont Chrysler Dodge Jeep Ram Cody** – *Cody, WY (Local / Nearby)*
* **Location:** 3127 Big Horn Avenue, Cody, WY 82414
* **Distance from Powell:** ~24 miles southwest (~25–30 minutes)
* **Why It’s a Top Choice:**
  * **Dedicated CDJR Facility:** Located just down the road in Cody, this is the dedicated Chrysler, Dodge, Jeep, and Ram hub for the Fremont network in Park County.
  * **Broader Immediate Stock:** It frequently carries additional new Ram configurations and Certified Pre-Owned (CPO) inventory on the lot that you can test-drive within a short drive from Powell.
  * **Seamless Coordination:** Because Powell and Cody share the same dealer ownership, pricing, financing, and trade-in valuations can easily be cross-coordinated between the two locations.
* **Things to Consider:** As with any single-group territory, cross-shopping between Cody and Powell will largely reflect the same ownership group's pricing policies.

Remember that Wyoming sales and use tax will still be collected when you register the vehicle at the Park County Treasurer's office. For routine maintenance and warranty service, you will likely prefer using your local Powell or Cody Mopar service center.
