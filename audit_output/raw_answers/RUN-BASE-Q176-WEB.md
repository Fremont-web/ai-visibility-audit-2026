Several car dealerships physically located in Wyoming offer end-to-end online buying platforms with direct vehicle delivery to your home or office, alongside regional and national online auto retailers.

### 2. Regional & National Online Alternatives

* **Carvana:** Delivers pre-owned vehicles directly to driveways across many Wyoming ZIP codes. If you live outside their immediate transport lanes, they may arrange drop-off at a central location or assess an extended delivery fee. Carvana offers a 7-day money-back guarantee.
* **CarMax:** Provides 100% online purchasing with home delivery available within specific mileage radii of regional hub stores (such as Denver/Northern Colorado or Salt Lake City). Vehicles can also be transferred across state lines for local pickup or direct delivery.
* **Front Range Colorado & Utah Dealerships:** Due to geographic proximity, several large dealership groups in Fort Collins, Loveland, Denver, and Salt Lake City regularly sell online to Wyoming residents and offer transport/trailer delivery across state lines.

### Practical Shopper Tips for Wyoming Online Car Buying

1. **Confirm Delivery Radius & Fees:** While some dealerships include local drop-offs for free, cross-county or remote deliveries in rural Wyoming often incur a per-mile or flat transport fee.
2. **Wyoming Sales Tax & Registration:** Wyoming collects vehicle sales/use taxes at the county treasurer's office rather than at the state level. Local Wyoming dealers will handle the tax paperwork or provide the necessary title paperwork for your specific county treasurer.
3. **Return & Inspection Windows:** Ask the dealer whether they offer a trial return window (e.g., 3–7 days) to ensure you are satisfied with the vehicle once it is unloaded on your driveway.
