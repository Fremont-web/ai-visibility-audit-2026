### Tips Before You Go

* **Check Open Recalls First:** Look up your 17-digit Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) on the official [Mopar Recall Tool](https://www.mopar.com/en-us/care/safety/recalls.html) or the [NHTSA Recall Database](https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls).
* **Verify Parts Inventory:** When scheduling recall service, provide your VIN so the service advisor can ensure necessary replacement parts are currently in stock before your appointment.
* **Independent Shops:** Independent auto repair shops in Rock Springs can handle standard maintenance, but they are not certified to process Dodge warranty reimbursement or complete federally mandated recall campaigns.
