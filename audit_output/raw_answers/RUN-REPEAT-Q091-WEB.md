### 4. What to Expect & Tips for a 4WD Truck Under $25,000 in Wyoming

1. **Realistic Age and Mileage:** In today's truck market, a dependable 4WD half-ton or three-quarter-ton truck priced under $25,000 will typically be roughly 8 to 15 years old (e.g., model years 2011–2017) with mileage generally ranging from 90,000 to 160,000+ miles.
2. **4WD System Inspection:** Test the 4WD system thoroughly—ensure 4-Hi and 4-Lo engage smoothly without grinding, and verify the transfer case, front differential, and CV boots/axles are in good condition.
3. **Undercarriage & Rust:** Inspect the frame, cab corners, rocker panels, and brake lines for corrosion caused by winter road magnesium chloride or salt.
4. **Check Online Aggregators & Classifieds:** Monitor online platforms like CarGurus, Autotrader, and Cars.com set to a 50-to-100-mile radius around Powell (ZIP 82435), as well as local private listings in the *Powell Tribune* classifieds and local community boards.
