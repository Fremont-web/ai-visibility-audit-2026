### Tips for Dodge Warranty & Recall Service
1. **Check Open Recalls First:** Look up your 17-digit Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) on [Mopar's Recall Lookup](https://www.mopar.com/en-us/care/safety/recalls.html) or the [NHTSA Recalls Database](https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls). Recalls are performed free of charge to you.
2. **Call Ahead for Parts Availability:** Many recall remedies require specific parts (such as replacement airbag inflators, fuel system components, or software update modules). Always call the service department with your VIN to confirm the recall parts are physically on the shelf before bringing the vehicle in.
3. **Warranty Documentation:** Bring your vehicle registration and any active third-party or extended warranty documentation, although factory warranties are automatically tracked by VIN through the Stellantis dealer network.
