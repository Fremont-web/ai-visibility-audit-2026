### Buyer's Checklist for Used Volkswagens in Wyoming

1. **Verify "As-Is" vs. Written Warranty:** Under Wyoming state law, used vehicles are sold strictly **"as is"** unless a specific written warranty or service contract is stated on the FTC Buyer's Guide and purchase agreement. Never rely on verbal promises.
2. **Factory CPO vs. Third-Party Service Contracts:** A factory Volkswagen CPO warranty is accepted at any authorized VW dealership across the United States with standard claim procedures and genuine parts. Third-party extended warranties vary widely—always check whether they require prior authorization, use aftermarket parts, or limit labor rates.
3. **Check Remaining Factory Warranty:** Recent-year Volkswagens may still have the remainder of their original 4-year / 50,000-mile factory warranty, which automatically transfers to subsequent owners at no additional cost.
4. **Get a Pre-Purchase Inspection (PPI):** Even when buying a car with a warranty, obtain an independent mechanic's inspection and review the vehicle history report (CARFAX/AutoCheck) for previous accidents, title brands, and regular maintenance records.
