### Practical Advice for Securing a Written OTD Quote in Park County, WY

1.  **Request a "Buyer’s Order" or "Signed Purchase Agreement":** When contacting sales reps by email or text, ask explicitly: *"Please email me an itemized Buyer's Order showing the complete out-the-door price including sales price, dealer documentation fee, any dealer-installed accessories, and government fees."*
2.  **Watch the Wyoming Doc Fee:** Wyoming law does **not** cap dealer doc fees; they range from $55 to over $1,000 across the state. In the Powell/Cody area, aim for dealers charging under $300 (such as Fremont Ford Cody at ~$85 or Denny Menholt Cody at ~$285) rather than dealers charging $500–$600+.
3.  **Taxes in Park County:** Dealerships may or may not roll Wyoming sales tax into the purchase price depending on whether you finance. In Wyoming, sales tax (4% state plus any local county general-purpose tax) is typically paid directly to the County Treasurer within 65 days of purchase unless collected by the dealer.
