### Important Shopper & Owner Guidance
* **Safety Recalls Are Free:** Official manufacturer safety recalls are performed at **no cost** to you for parts and labor, regardless of vehicle mileage, warranty expiration status, or where you originally purchased the truck.
* **Check Your VIN in Advance:** Before making an appointment, look up any open recalls on the [Mopar Recall Portal](https://www.mopar.com/en-us/care/safety/recalls.html) or the [NHTSA Recall Website](https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls) using your 17-digit Vehicle Identification Number (VIN).
* **Call Ahead to Reserve Parts:** Certain recall remedies require specific replacement modules, hardware, or software updates. Providing your VIN to the service department prior to your visit ensures that the required Mopar replacement parts are on hand before you bring your truck in.
