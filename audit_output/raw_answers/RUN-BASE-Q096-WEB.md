### Practical Steps Before You Go

* **Check Open Recalls First:** Look up your vehicle's 17-digit Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) on the [Ford Recall Portal](https://www.ford.com/support/how-tos/recall/recalls-and-faqs/how-can-i-confirm-if-my-vehicle-has-a-recall/) or the [NHTSA Recalls Database](https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls).
* **Call Ahead to Verify Parts:** Recall remedies often require specific parts (such as replacement inflators, sensors, or wiring harnesses). Provide your VIN when scheduling so the service department can confirm parts are physically in stock before you bring the car in.
* **Ask About Mobile Service:** Inquire whether your specific recall can be performed via mobile dispatch to avoid a visit to the dealership.
