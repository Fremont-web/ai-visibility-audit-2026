### Practical Recommendations for Shoppers
1. **Check for Remaining Factory Warranty:** Most Ram 1500 gas trucks come from the factory with a 5-year/60,000-mile powertrain warranty, while 2500/3500 Cummins diesel trucks have a 5-year/100,000-mile powertrain warranty. If you purchase a truck under 5 years old within those mileage limits, the balance transfers automatically.
2. **Clarify CPO vs. Service Contract:** Ask explicitly whether a truck is "Manufacturer Certified Pre-Owned" (honored at any CDJR dealer nationwide) or covered under a dealer/third-party extended service contract. Review deductibles (typically $100 for Ram CPO repairs) and excluded wear items.
3. **Inspect Vehicle History:** Request the AutoCheck or CARFAX report to verify routine maintenance and ensure there are no previous frame damage or salvage title records that would void warranty eligibility.
