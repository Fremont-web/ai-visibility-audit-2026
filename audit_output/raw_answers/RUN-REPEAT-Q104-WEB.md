If you want to sell your vehicle in or around Sheridan, Wyoming, without committing to buying another car, you have several solid options ranging from local franchise lots right in town to larger regional dealership networks in Gillette and Billings.

Most modern dealerships actively purchase pre-owned vehicles outright to maintain their used vehicle inventories, and several have structured programs specifically designed for cash buyouts.

### What to Bring When Selling Your Vehicle

To complete an outright sale to any dealership, bring:
1. **Vehicle Title:** Must be in your name. If you still have an outstanding auto loan, bring your lender's name, account number, and an official 10-day loan payoff statement.
2. **All Registered Owners:** All individuals listed on the title must be present with valid government-issued photo IDs (or provide documented Power of Attorney).
3. **Current Vehicle Registration**.
4. **All Keys, Remotes, and Accessories:** Leaving remotes or extra keys behind can lower the final appraisal payout.

*Tip:* Dealers base outright purchase offers on wholesale market values. Getting appraisals from at least two local dealerships (such as Fremont Ford/Toyota and Sheridan Motors) gives you an objective baseline to ensure you receive the strongest offer.
