### Consumer Tips Before Scheduling Your Visit

1. **Check Open Recalls First:** Look up your 17-digit Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) on [Toyota's Official Recall Lookup](https://www.toyota.com/recall) or the [NHTSA Recalls Database](https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls) to confirm open safety campaigns.
2. **Call Ahead with Your VIN:** Recall campaigns frequently require specific parts or remedy kits. Calling the service department before making an appointment ensures the necessary parts are physically on-site before you bring your vehicle in.
3. **Warranty Documentation:** For standard factory warranty or ToyotaCare scheduled services, bring your vehicle registration and any relevant maintenance records.
