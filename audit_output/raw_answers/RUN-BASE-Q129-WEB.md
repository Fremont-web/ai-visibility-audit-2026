Casper, Wyoming has several reputable options for purchasing a used vehicle, split between well-established independent used car dealers and major franchised dealerships offering certified pre-owned (CPO) programs. ### Practical Tips for Buying a Used Car in Casper

1.  **Inspect for Undercarriage Wear & Rust:** Wyoming winters involve road sand, salt, and de-icing chemicals. Always inspect the underside of used vehicles for corrosion, especially on used trucks and older SUVs.
2.  **Verify the Vehicle History Report:** Ask for a CARFAX or AutoCheck report to check for title brandings, accident history, and service intervals.
3.  **Request an Independent Pre-Purchase Inspection (PPI):** Reputable dealers in Casper will allow you to take the vehicle to a trusted third-party mechanic for a standard inspection before closing the sale.
4.  **Get a Written Out-the-Door (OTD) Quote:** Ask for an itemized breakdown including purchase price, state/county sales tax, title/registration, and dealer documentation fees before agreeing to financing terms.
