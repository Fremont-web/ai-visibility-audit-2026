When shopping for a Dodge vehicle in or around Cody, Wyoming, options within the immediate town are limited due to the rural geography of the Bighorn Basin. However, local buyers have a dedicated franchise right in town, a close sister dealership in neighboring Powell, and high-volume regional alternatives within driving distance.

Here are the top Dodge dealership options in and near Cody, along with why each may suit your needs:

---

### 1. Fremont Chrysler Dodge Jeep Ram (Cody, WY) – *Best for Local Convenience & Ongoing Service*
* **Location:** 3127 Big Horn Ave, Cody, WY 82414
* **Distance:** In-town
* **Why It’s a Top Pick:**
  * **Hometown Location:** As the only authorized CDJR (Chrysler, Dodge, Jeep, Ram) dealer physically located in Cody, it is the most convenient choice for sales consultations, routine maintenance, recall work, and factory warranty repairs.
  * **Statewide Network Transfers:** Fremont Motor Companies operates multiple dealerships across Wyoming (including Casper, Lander, and Rock Springs). If the Cody lot does not have the exact Dodge trim or package you want (e.g., a specific Durango or Hornet), they can often arrange dealer transfers across their network.
  * **Certified Mopar Service:** Staffed with factory-trained technicians specializing in Stellantis/Dodge vehicles and genuine Mopar parts.

### 2. Fremont Motor Powell (Powell, WY) – *Best Nearby Alternative*

* **Distance:** ~100 miles north of Cody (~1.75 to 2 hours drive via WY-120 and US-310/US-212)
