### Shopper Checklist for Buying a Used Ram with a Warranty
1. **Check for Remaining Factory Warranty:** Any Ram truck under 5 years old and under 60,000 miles (gas) or 100,000 miles (Cummins Turbo Diesel) may still carry transferable original factory powertrain coverage at no extra charge.
2. **Confirm CPO vs. Dealer Warranty:** Ensure whether the vehicle is an official Stellantis Ram CPO truck (valid at any CDJR dealer nationwide) or has a dealer-specific/third-party warranty (which may require returning to the selling dealership or paying higher deductibles).
3. **Request the Inspection & CARFAX Report:** Review the multi-point inspection sheet and ensure there is no prior frame or flood damage that would void warranty eligibility.
4. **Get Coverage in Writing:** Always ensure the warranty terms, duration, mileage limits, and deductible are written into the final purchase agreement.
