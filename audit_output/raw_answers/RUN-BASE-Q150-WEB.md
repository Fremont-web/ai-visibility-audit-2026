## Important Wyoming Out-the-Door Buying Tips

1. **Wyoming Vehicle Sales & Use Tax is Paid at the County Courthouse:**
   In Wyoming (including Sweetwater County), vehicle sales and use tax (typically 5%–6%) is usually collected directly by the **County Treasurer** within 60 to 65 days of purchase, not at the dealership—unless you are financing the taxes into your auto loan. Clarify with the dealer whether their "out-the-door" figure includes county sales tax or strictly represents **Vehicle Price + Dealer Doc Fee + Temp Tag/Filing Fees**.

2. **Documentation Fees Are Uncapped in Wyoming:**
   Wyoming does not set a statutory maximum for dealership doc fees. Rock Springs fees range from **$374** at Whisler Chevrolet to **$599–$634** at Brower Brothers Nissan and Fremont CDJR. While dealerships generally will not remove the doc fee line item, you can negotiate an equivalent discount on the vehicle selling price.

3. **How to Request a Binding Written Quote:**
   When contacting any dealership's Internet/BDC department, state:
   > *"Please email me an itemized Purchase Agreement/Buyer's Order for Stock #[number] showing the vehicle selling price, dealer documentation fee, all dealer-installed accessories, and government fees. I am ready to purchase once I review a complete written out-the-door number."*
