For factory warranty repairs, Cummins diesel maintenance, and authorized safety recall service on a Ram truck near Lander, Wyoming, repairs must be performed at an authorized Ram / Stellantis dealership.

### Primary Authorized Service Center:
* **Fremont Motor Riverton (Fremont CDJR Riverton):**
  * **Location:** 1601 N Federal Blvd, Riverton, WY 82501 (approx. 24–25 miles northeast of Lander)
  * **Services:** Factory-certified Ram/Cummins heavy-duty technicians, genuine OEM Mopar parts, heavy commercial vehicle lifts, and free manufacturer recall completion.
  * **Why here:** There is no franchised Ram dealership or certified warranty facility located inside Lander. Fremont CDJR in Riverton serves as the direct franchise service center for Fremont County.

### Regional Alternatives:

* **Fremont CDJR Cody:** ~150 miles north in Park County.
