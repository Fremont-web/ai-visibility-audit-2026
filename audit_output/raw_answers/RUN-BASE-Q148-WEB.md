### Helpful Tips for Recall & Warranty Appointments

1. **Verify Open Recalls Prior to Scheduling:** Check your 17-digit Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) on the [NHTSA Recall Website](https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls) or the [Mopar Owner Portal](https://www.mopar.com) to confirm the exact campaign numbers.
2. **Call Ahead with Your VIN:** Certain recall campaigns require specific parts or software calibration tools that may need to be ordered in advance. Providing your VIN when scheduling ensures the dealership has parts on hand before you bring in your truck.
3. **Independent Mechanics Cannot Perform Warranty/Recall Claims:** While third-party shops can handle regular maintenance, warranty and recall work must go through an authorized CDJR dealer to be covered under factory warranty terms.
