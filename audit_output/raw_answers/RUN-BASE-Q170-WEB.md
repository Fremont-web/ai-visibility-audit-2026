## 3. Critical Wyoming "Out-the-Door" Caveat: Sales Tax & Registration

When evaluating an out-the-door price in Wyoming, keep this legal requirement in mind:

* **Dealerships Do Not Collect Sales Tax:** Unlike many other states, Wyoming dealerships do **not** collect state or county vehicle sales and use taxes at the point of sale.
* **Paid to the County Treasurer:** By state law, the buyer must pay vehicle sales/use tax directly to their home **County Treasurer** (for Rawlins residents, the Carbon County Treasurer's Office) within **65 days** of vehicle purchase when titling and licensing the vehicle.
* **What the Dealership's Written OTD Quote Covers:**
  1. Agreed Vehicle Sale Price (MSRP minus dealer discounts and rebates)
  2. Dealer Documentation Fee ($0 to $599 depending on dealer)
  3. Any agreed dealer accessories or protection packages
  4. Temporary tag / transit permit fee (nominal, usually $5–$20)

To know your true total cost of ownership, remember to calculate your county sales tax rate (typically 5%–6% depending on county of residence) and local plate/registration fees separate from the dealership check.

## 4. Tips for Getting a Binding Written Quote

1. **Ask Specifically for a "Signed Buyer's Order" or "Purchase Agreement":** Rather than accepting an informal email note or verbal quote, ask the sales manager or internet director to send a scanned or PDF Buyer's Order.
2. **Specify "Zero Add-Ons":** When asking for the quote, state: *"Please send me an itemized out-the-door buyer's order for stock #[XYZ], including the vehicle sale price, all eligible rebates, and your dealer documentation fee, with all optional dealer-installed accessories or protection packages removed."*
3. **Lock It In Before Traveling:** Before driving 100+ miles along I-80 or WY-220, have the dealer confirm in writing that the quoted price does not require purchasing mandatory in-house financing or unlisted protection plans.
