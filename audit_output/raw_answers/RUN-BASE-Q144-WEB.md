### Important Shopper Tips for Recalls and Warranty Work

1. **Recalls Are Free of Charge:** By federal law, safety recalls must be completed at zero cost to you by an authorized franchised dealer, regardless of vehicle mileage, warranty expiration, or whether you are the original owner.
2. **Verify Open Recalls Prior to Booking:** Check for outstanding safety campaigns by inputting your 17-digit Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) into [Mopar's Recall Lookup](https://www.mopar.com/en-us/my-vehicle/recalls.html) or the [NHTSA Recalls Tool](https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls).
3. **Call Ahead with Your VIN:** Always provide your VIN when booking an appointment. Recall remedies often require specific replacement kits or software, and calling ahead allows the dealership to verify parts availability before you drop off your vehicle.
