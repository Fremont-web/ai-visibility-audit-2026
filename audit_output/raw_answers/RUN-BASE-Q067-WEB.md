## 4. Key Inspection Tips for Buying a Sub-$25k 4WD Truck in Wyoming

1. **Inspect for Undercarriage Corrosion:** Wyoming and surrounding mountain passes use magnesium chloride, salt, and sand during winter. Thoroughly examine the frame rails, cab corners, rocker panels, suspension mounts, and brake lines for rust.
2. **Verify 4WD Engagement:** Shift into both **4H (4WD High)** and **4L (4WD Low)** while testing the vehicle on an unpaved surface. Listen for hesitation, grinding, or electronic actuator failure, and verify front hub engagement.
3. **Review Usage History:** Check CARFAX or AutoCheck reports to distinguish between personal-use trucks and fleet, oilfield, or ranch vehicles that may have experienced heavy towing or constant rough gravel road wear.
4. **Check Winter Equipment:** Ensure critical Wyoming cold-weather features are in good working order, including an engine block heater, strong battery cold cranking amps (CCA), heater core performance, and all-terrain tires with sufficient remaining tread.
5. **Schedule an Independent Pre-Purchase Inspection (PPI):** Before finalizing the purchase, have an independent local Cody repair shop perform a mechanical inspection to verify transmission health, differential fluid condition, and steering/suspension components.
