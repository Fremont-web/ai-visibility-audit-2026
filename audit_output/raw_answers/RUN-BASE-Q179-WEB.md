### Overview: Dealer Groups and Warranty Rules in Wyoming

When evaluating multi-city dealership groups in Wyoming for warranty work, it is important to distinguish between **Manufacturer (OEM) Factory Warranty & Recall Work** and **Dealer-Specific / Extended Service Contracts**:

* **OEM Factory Warranty & Safety Recalls:** Under automaker franchise agreements and federal law, factory warranty repairs and official recalls can **only** be performed by a service department that holds the specific OEM franchise. Even within the same dealer ownership group, a Ford-branded rooftop cannot perform factory warranty repairs on a Ram or Toyota, and vice versa.
* **Dealer Group Programs (e.g., Fremont Care):** Dealer-specific perks, complimentary scheduled maintenance, or in-house certified pre-owned limited warranties are typically honored across all service departments within that ownership family.
* **Third-Party Extended Warranties (VSCs):** Independent vehicle service contracts (e.g., Zurich, Ally, CNA, Endurance) can generally be serviced at any ASE-certified service department within a group, provided the warranty administrator pre-authorizes the repairs.
