### Important Consumer Guidelines for Recalls & Warranty Work
* **Safety Recalls Are Free:** By federal law, authorized CDJR dealers must perform all safety recall repairs completely free of charge, regardless of your vehicle's mileage, warranty status, or whether you bought the vehicle new or used.
* **Independent Shops Cannot Bill Recalls/Warranty:** Independent used car dealers (such as Whitlock Motors in Cody) or general mechanic shops cannot process factory warranty claims or perform official recall campaign sign-offs.
* **Check Your VIN First:** You can verify active open recalls prior to scheduling by entering your 17-character Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) at [mopar.com/en-us/my-vehicle/recalls.html](https://www.mopar.com/en-us/my-vehicle/recalls.html) or [nhtsa.gov/recalls](https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls).
* **Call Ahead to Reserve Parts:** When scheduling recall work, provide your VIN and the specific recall campaign code to ensure the dealership has the required replacement parts in stock before you bring your vehicle in.
