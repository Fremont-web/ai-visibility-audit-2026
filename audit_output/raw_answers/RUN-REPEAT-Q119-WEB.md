### Key Tips for Buying a Used Ram with a Warranty in Wyoming

1. **Verify Factory Remaining Coverage:** Ram trucks carry a 5-year/60,000-mile factory powertrain warranty on gas engines (3.6L Pentastar and 5.7L Hemi) and a 5-year/100,000-mile powertrain warranty on Cummins diesel models. If buying a truck that is 2–4 years old, the remaining factory balance automatically transfers to you at no extra cost.
2. **Powertrain vs. Comprehensive (Bumper-to-Bumper):** Ensure you understand whether the warranty covers only internally lubricated engine and transmission parts or covers common electrical and suspension components (e.g., air suspension, Uconnect touchscreen systems, transfer cases).
3. **Inspect Vehicle History for Wyoming Duty:** Many trucks in Central Wyoming have seen oilfield, ranching, or heavy hauling use. Always review the CARFAX or AutoCheck report for regular maintenance intervals and rust/undercarriage condition from winter highway treatments.
