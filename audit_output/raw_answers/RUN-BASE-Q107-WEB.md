### Key Tips & Shopper Caveats for Buying in Wyoming

1. **No Wyoming Used-Car Lemon Law:** In Wyoming, used vehicles are sold strictly "as is" unless a written warranty or service contract is explicitly provided by the seller.
2. **Examine the FTC Buyer's Guide:** Check the window sticker on any vehicle you inspect to confirm whether it is marked **"Warranty"** or **"As Is - No Dealer Warranty."**
3. **Identify the Type of Coverage:**
   * *Manufacturer CPO:* Accepted at any CDJR dealership nationwide with standardized factory backing.
   * *Dealer-Group Limited Warranty:* Often tied to the specific dealer group (e.g., Fremont Motors).
   * *Third-Party Vehicle Service Contract:* Requires you to understand deductibles, claim authorization rules, and covered components before signing.
4. **Check Remaining Factory Warranty:** Late-model used Chryslers may still be covered under the original 3-year/36,000-mile basic or 5-year/60,000-mile powertrain manufacturer warranty, which transfers automatically to subsequent owners without extra cost.
