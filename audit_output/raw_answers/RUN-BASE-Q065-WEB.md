### Buyer Tips for Shopping Used Cars in Cody
1. **Inspect for Winter Wear:** Check vehicle undercarriages, frame rails, and suspension components for road salt corrosion or gravel chips common to Rocky Mountain driving.
2. **Verify Vehicle History:** Always request a CARFAX or AutoCheck report to confirm service history, prior ownership, and whether the vehicle was previously a rental or fleet vehicle.
3. **Utilize Dealer Networks:** Franchised dealerships like Fremont and Denny Menholt can often transfer pre-owned vehicles from their sister lots across Wyoming and Montana at minimal or no charge.
4. **Get an Independent Pre-Purchase Inspection (PPI):** Even when buying from an established lot, having an independent mechanic inspect the vehicle before finalizing purchase is recommended.
