### Shopper Checklist for Used 4WD Trucks in Wyoming
1. **Verify True 4WD vs. AWD:** Ensure the truck has a selectable two-speed transfer case (with 4-High and 4-Low settings), which is critical for deep snow, ice, and unpaved Wyoming roads.
2. **Inspect the Frame and Undercarriage:** Inspect the frame rails, cab corners, rocker panels, and brake/fuel lines for severe rust or corrosion from road de-icing chemicals (magnesium chloride).
3. **Test the 4WD System:** During the test drive, shift into 4-Hi and 4-Lo on an unpaved surface to ensure the transfer case actuator engages smoothly without grinding, clunking, or warning lights.
4. **Check Known Engine & Transmission Issues:** Common trucks in this price bracket include the Ford 5.0L Coyote or 3.5L EcoBoost (check for timing chain/cam phaser rattle), Ram 5.7L Hemi (listen for lifter/exhaust manifold ticks), and GM 5.3L Vortec/EcoTec3 (check transmission shifting and AFM lifter history).
5. **Get a Pre-Purchase Inspection (PPI):** Have a trusted, independent Casper mechanic inspect the vehicle on a lift before finalizing the transaction.
