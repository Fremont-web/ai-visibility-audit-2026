### Important Advice for Jeep Warranty & Recall Repairs

1. **Verify Open Recalls in Advance:** Locate your 17-digit Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) on your dashboard or vehicle registration. Check its status on the official [Mopar Recall Lookup](https://www.mopar.com/en-us/my-vehicle/recalls.html) or the NHTSA recall tool.
2. **Call Ahead Before Bringing Your Jeep In:** Recall repairs are performed **100% free of charge**, but many recall campaigns require specific parts kits (e.g., fuel pump modules, software reprogrammers, or wiring harnesses). Provide your VIN when calling the service department so they can confirm part availability or order the parts before your appointment.
3. **Independent Mechanics Note:** Independent garages (such as Whitlock Motors in Cody or local repair shops) are suitable for general maintenance, but they cannot bill Jeep/Stellantis for warranty repairs or sign off on NHTSA recall completions.
