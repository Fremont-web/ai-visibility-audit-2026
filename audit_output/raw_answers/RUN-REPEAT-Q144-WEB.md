### Important Guidance for Warranty & Recall Service

1. **Authorized Dealer Requirement:** Independent automotive repair shops cannot perform manufacturer safety recalls or file factory warranty claims with Stellantis/Jeep. The work must be performed by a certified Mopar technician at an authorized dealership to be covered at no charge.
2. **Free Recall Service:** Safety recalls mandated by NHTSA or issued by Stellantis/Jeep are performed at zero cost to the owner for parts and labor.
3. **Check Open Recalls First:** Look up your vehicle by its 17-character Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) on [Mopar.com Recalls](https://www.mopar.com) or the [NHTSA Recall Database](https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls) before scheduling.
4. **Call Ahead for Parts Availability:** Some recall remedies require specific replacement kits or parts that may need to be pre-ordered by the dealership prior to your appointment date.
