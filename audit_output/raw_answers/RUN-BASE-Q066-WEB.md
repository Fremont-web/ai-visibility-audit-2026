### Important Wyoming Car-Buying Caveat: Sales Tax & Registration
When requesting an out-the-door price in Park County, make sure you clarify how taxes are handled:
* In Wyoming, **vehicle sales tax is paid directly to the County Treasurer** (Park County Treasurer in Cody) at the time of registration (within 65 days of purchase), **not** retained by the dealer.
* If you are paying cash or bringing outside financing, a dealer's "out-the-door" price might reflect only the vehicle selling price, dealer doc fee, and transit/title fee.
* If you intend to finance the sales tax into your auto loan, explicitly tell the dealership to calculate and include the **Park County sales tax** on the written buyer's agreement so the lender can remit the payment to the county on your behalf.
