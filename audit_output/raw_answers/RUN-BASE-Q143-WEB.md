### Practical Advice for Buying a Used Jeep with a Warranty
1. **Distinguish Factory CPO from Dealer/Third-Party Warranties:** A manufacturer-backed Stellantis CPO warranty is honored at any authorized Jeep dealership nationwide. Dealer-specific or aftermarket warranties may have restrictions regarding where repair work must be performed or impose deductibles.
2. **Inspect for Aftermarket Modifications & Off-Road Use:** Jeeps are commonly modified with suspension lift kits, oversized tires, and winch bumpers. Confirm whether existing modifications impact the warranty, as powertrain claims can be denied if aftermarket parts caused mechanical failure.
3. **Request Vehicle History & Common Wear Checkpoints:** Insist on a clean CARFAX/AutoCheck. On models equipped with the 3.6L Pentastar V6, check the oil filter housing and cooling system for signs of leakage. For Wranglers, ensure the steering and suspension have been thoroughly inspected to eliminate front-end play or steering wobble.
