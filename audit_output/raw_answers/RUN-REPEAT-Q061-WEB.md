**Question:** What are the best Ram dealerships in or near Cody, Wyoming, and why?

**SHA-256 Hash:** `None`

---

For truck shoppers in Cody, Wyoming, selecting the right Ram dealership comes down to proximity, inventory size, and long-term service convenience. Because Wyoming's rugged terrain and winter weather demand reliable heavy-duty performance, choosing a reputable dealer for both purchase and ongoing maintenance is critical.

Here is an objective guide distinguishing local dealerships in the Big Horn Basin from regional alternatives within driving distance:

---

### Local Dealerships (Cody & Immediate Big Horn Basin)

#### 1. Fremont Chrysler Dodge Jeep Ram Cody
* **Location:** 3127 Big Horn Ave, Cody, WY 82414
* **Why It’s a Top Choice:** It is the only factory-authorized Ram franchise physically located in Cody. For local residents, having sales, warranty work, and Mopar-certified service directly in town provides unmatched convenience.
* **Key Strengths:**
  * **Inventory & Upfits:** Full lineup of Ram 1500, 2500, and 3500 trucks, as well as commercial chassis cabs and work-ready upfits.
  * **Fremont Care:** New and qualifying pre-owned vehicles typically come with Fremont Care (covering initial routine maintenance such as oil changes and tire rotations during the first year).
  * **Dealer Network Access:** As part of the statewide Fremont Motor Company family, they can transfer Ram vehicles from sister lots across Wyoming without requiring the buyer to travel.
  * **Reputation:** Consistently earns strong local reviews (~4.7/5 stars), with frequent praise for helpful parts personnel and accommodating emergency service for travelers and locals.

#### 2. Fremont Motor Powell (Chrysler Dodge Jeep Ram Ford)
* **Location:** 1000 US Highway 14 Alternate, Powell, WY 82435 (~24 miles northeast of Cody)
* **Why It’s a Top Choice:** Located just a 25-minute drive from Cody, Fremont Motor Powell serves as the immediate sister store to the Cody location. Powell carries both CDJR and Ford franchises under one roof.
* **Key Strengths:**
  * **High Customer Satisfaction:** Highly regarded in the Basin, boasting an average rating of ~4.8/5 stars across Google, Facebook, and DealerRater.
  * **Convenient Second Local Lot:** If Cody doesn't have the specific cab configuration, axle ratio, or Cummins turbo-diesel trim you need on the lot, Powell often has complementary stock nearby.
  * **Cross-Brand Comparison:** Shoppers deciding between a Ram truck and a Ford F-Series can evaluate and test-drive both brands side-by-side at the same dealership.

### Regional Alternatives (Broader Driving Distance)

For buyers seeking deeper volume discounts or specific, hard-to-find commercial configurations not in local stock, two regional hubs are worth considering:

#### 3. Lithia Chrysler Jeep Dodge RAM of Billings
* **Location:** 2229 King Ave W, Billings, MT 59102 (~105 miles north of Cody; approx. 1 hour 45 minutes)
* **Why It’s a Contender:** Billings is the largest automotive market in the Northern Rockies. As a high-volume metropolitan dealer, Lithia frequently stocks an extensive inventory of Ram 1500s and heavy-duty trucks.
* **Key Strengths:**
  * **Selection:** Greater variety of trim levels (Tradesman, Big Horn, Laramie, Rebel, Limited, and TRX/RHO) and commercial configurations.
  * **Competitive Pricing:** Higher allocation and inventory turnover often translate to aggressive discount incentives and trade-in flexibility.
  * **Buyer Consideration:** While purchase trips to Billings are common for Park County residents, remember that future warranty work, recalls, and routine servicing will require either traveling back to Billings or booking through local Cody/Powell service departments.

#### 4. Sheridan Motors (Chrysler Dodge Jeep Ram)
* **Location:** 3270 Wrench Dr, Sheridan, WY 82801 (~145 miles east of Cody; approx. 2.5 to 3 hours)
* **Why It’s a Contender:** Located across the Bighorn Mountains, Sheridan Motors is an established full-line CDJR dealership serving northern Wyoming.
* **Key Strengths:**
  * **Customer Feedback:** Maintains strong reviews (~4.7/5 stars on Carfax) with positive feedback on sales transparency and Mopar service.
  * **In-State Comparison:** An independent Wyoming alternative to the Fremont group, useful for shoppers who prefer to keep their purchase within Wyoming dealer networks.
  * **Buyer Consideration:** Travel via US-14 over Granite Pass can be hazardous or closed during winter storms, making seasonal timing and trip planning essential.

### Practical Tips for Cody Ram Shoppers
1. **Check Cross-Lot Availability First:** Before making the 2-hour drive to Montana or Sheridan, check with Fremont Cody or Powell. They can often arrange internal dealer-trades within their network to bring a truck to Cody.
2. **Sales Tax & Titling:** If you purchase in Montana (which has no state sales tax), you will still pay Wyoming state and Park County sales/use tax when registering and titling the vehicle at the Park County Courthouse in Cody.
3. **Diesel & Winter Prep:** Wyoming winters place heavy demands on truck blocks, batteries, and fuel filters. Confirm that any truck purchased includes cold-weather essentials (engine block heaters, winter front covers, and heavy-duty alternators).
