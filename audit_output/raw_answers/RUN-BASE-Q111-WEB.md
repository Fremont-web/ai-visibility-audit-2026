### Key Tips for Buying a Reliable Used Dodge with a Warranty

1. **Verify the Warranty Type:** Factory-backed Dodge CPO warranties are honored at any CDJR dealership across the country with standardized coverage. Dealer-specific or third-party service contracts have varying deductible levels, repair facility limitations, and claim approval processes.
2. **Check the In-Service Date:** For CPO vehicles, the 7-year/100,000-mile powertrain warranty starts on the date the vehicle was first sold new—not the date you buy it. Ask the dealer for the vehicle's original in-service date to see exactly how much coverage remains.
3. **Review History & Mechanical Common Points:** Request the full CARFAX/AutoCheck. On popular Dodge models like the Durango or Charger, check maintenance records for regular fluid services (transmission and transfer case for AWD models) and listen for common mechanical items like HEMI exhaust manifold leaks or suspension bushing wear from harsh Wyoming winter driving.
4. **Get Written Warranty Terms:** Always confirm the deductible (often $100 per visit on CPO), what components are excluded (wear-and-tear vs. mechanical breakdown), and whether roadside assistance and towing are included.
