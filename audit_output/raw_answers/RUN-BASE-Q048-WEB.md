### Practical Tips Before You Schedule
1. **Locate Your VIN:** Have your 17-character Vehicle Identification Number ready (found on your dashboard, driver's door jamb, or vehicle registration/insurance card).
2. **Verify Open Recalls Online:** You can confirm active recalls directly on [Ford's Official Recall Site](https://www.ford.com/support/) or via the NHTSA recall lookup database.
3. **Call Ahead for Parts:** Many recall campaigns (such as software updates vs. hardware replacements like airbags or fuel modules) require specific OEM parts. Calling the dealership with your VIN ahead of time ensures parts are physically in stock before you bring the vehicle in.
4. **Inquire About Loaners / Shuttle Service:** If your warranty or recall repair requires an extended stay, ask the service advisor in advance about courtesy shuttle services or warranty-covered loaner vehicle availability.
